Artificial grass

Artificial grass is a genuinely good answer for a small shad.

Artificial grass is a genuinely good answer for a small shaded garden, a dog run or a family lawn that never gets a chance to recover. It is a poor answer for a big open garden, and we will tell you if we think that is what you have.

Almost all the problems with it come from the base. Laid on soil it ripples, laid without proper edges it lifts, and joined badly it shows a line down the middle for ever.

We build it on a compacted base with a laying course, fix every edge and join it so you cannot see where one roll ends.

Questions

Things people ask us

Does artificial grass get hot in summer?
In full sun it does warm up, more than real grass. Worth thinking about if the garden is south facing and used by young children.
Is artificial grass suitable for dogs?
Fine, with the right base and infill. It needs hosing occasionally, which we will explain.
How long does artificial grass last?
Ten to fifteen years for a good quality grass laid properly. The base is what determines whether it gets there.
How much does artificial grass cost to install?
Artificial grass is priced after the free visit as one fixed figure for the whole job, because the base usually costs more than the grass. What moves the price is the area, how deep the old lawn and soil have to be dug out, the sub-base and laying course, edge restraints, the grade of grass you pick, waste from awkward shapes and how materials get in. A low per-metre rate that leaves out the base is usually why a lawn ripples later.
What base does artificial grass need?
Artificial grass needs a dug-out, compacted, free-draining base: typically the old turf and soil removed to around 75–100mm, a compacted crushed stone sub-base, a fine laying course screeded level and a weed membrane. The edges are fixed to a timber, steel or concrete restraint so they cannot lift. Laid straight onto soil or old lawn, it ripples, holds puddles and grows weeds at the joins.
Does artificial grass drain on clay soil?
The grass itself drains through holes or a fully permeable backing, but on Milton Keynes clay the water then has to get through the base and into the ground, which is slow. On heavy clay the base is designed to shed water, often with a slight fall and sometimes a land drain, so rain does not sit under the lawn after a downpour. That matters even more with dogs, because a base that does not drain will smell.
Can artificial grass be laid on concrete or an old patio?
Yes, artificial grass can go over sound concrete or paving with a foam shock pad underneath to hide the joints and soften the surface. The existing surface needs a fall, and drainage holes where water could pond, and the edges need fixing so the grass cannot creep. Cracked or rocking slabs should be dealt with first, as any movement shows straight through the grass.
How do you clean artificial grass and stop it smelling with dogs?
Pick up solids, hose the area regularly and use an enzyme-based artificial grass cleaner every few weeks in warm weather to break down the urine that causes the smell. A free-draining base matters most, because urine that cannot drain away sits under the grass. Brushing against the pile now and then keeps the fibres upright, and a leaf blower clears debris without disturbing the infill.
Do weeds grow through artificial grass?
Very few weeds grow through artificial grass if it is laid on a weed membrane over a compacted base. The odd weed or moss that does appear usually grows on top, from seeds blown in and lodged in the infill or along the edges, and pulls straight out. Weeds coming up through the joins are a sign the joins were not made properly or the base was left as soil.
Can artificial grass melt from window reflections?
Yes, artificial grass can melt where sunlight is reflected and concentrated by some double or triple glazing, particularly energy-efficient glass with a slight bow, leaving a scorched line or patch. A barbecue, a dropped cigarette or a fire pit placed too close will do the same. If the lawn faces the sun and sits close to large glazing, we look for it on the visit and can suggest layout changes or window film.
Is artificial grass bad for the environment?
Artificial grass is plastic, so it gives nothing to insects or birds, gets hot in full sun and is hard to recycle at the end of its life. Against that, it suits spaces where real grass will never work, such as deep shade, heavy dog use or a tiny courtyard. Our usual advice is to keep a real lawn where it can thrive and use artificial grass only where it genuinely solves a problem.
How long does it take to lay artificial grass?
Most gardens take one to three days to lay artificial grass: digging out and taking away the old lawn and soil, building and compacting the base, then laying, joining and fixing the grass. Larger lawns, awkward shapes or access only through the house add time, and the dates are given to you in writing with the fixed price.
