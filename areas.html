Across the Midlands as standard

Travel is included anywhere within 50 miles of Corby. That takes in most of the East and West Midlands, including Leicester, Coventry, Nottingham, Northampton, Peterborough and Lincolnshire's southern towns, plus Milton Keynes, Bedford and Cambridge.

Anywhere else in the UK

We take on work across the country, from a single road scan to a rolling programme of plot checks on a large site. Travel beyond 50 miles is priced separately and shown on the quote, and grouping visits keeps it down.

Coverage in short: based in Corby. Travel included within 50 miles, which covers most of the Midlands. Anywhere else in the UK on request, with travel charged separately.

Questions we get asked

Where is Dimensure based?

Corby, Northamptonshire, right in the middle of the country. Everywhere within 50 miles of Corby is covered as standard, which includes most of the Midlands.

Do you travel further than the areas listed?

Yes, anywhere in the UK. Travel beyond 50 miles of Corby is priced in the quote and shown separately.

Is the price the same in every area?

The guide prices are the same everywhere. The only difference is travel for sites more than 50 miles from Corby, and batching visits keeps that low.
