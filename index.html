Questions

Things people ask us

Is the design really free?
Yes. The visit, the samples and the design all come free and there is no obligation afterwards. We would rather spend an hour showing you properly than talk you into something you are not sure about.
How long does a driveway take?
Most front drives in Milton Keynes are done inside a week. We will give you the actual dates in writing before you commit, and we do not start your job and disappear onto another one.
Will the price change once you have started?
No. The figure we agree is the figure you pay. Breaking out the old surface and base is priced in from the survey. The only thing that can move the figure is something nobody could have seen on the day — an old concrete slab buried under a lawn, say. If that happens we stop, show you, and agree the cost in writing before we carry on.
Will you leave a skip outside my house?
No. Everything goes away by grab lorry on the day it comes out, so there is no skip on your road for three weeks and no permit to pay for.
What happens if something goes wrong afterwards?
Ring the mobile number you already have. We come and look within five working days. Five year guarantee, in writing, and it transfers if you sell the house.
