Towcester specifically

Homes, ground and conservation in Towcester

Towcester is a Watling Street coaching town of ironstone, limestone and red brick built right up to the pavement, with yards behind, suburban streets where parking has taken over many front gardens, and the 2,750-home Towcester South extension to the south.

The old town sits on a clay spur of Upper Lias between the Tove and Silverstone Brook, with limestone and Northampton Sand on the valley sides and boulder clay higher up. In the centre expect slow-draining clay; on the slopes drainage is better.

Towcester Conservation Area has a 1998 Article 4 direction on frontages to Watling Street, Moat Lane, Park Street and nearby streets. It removes permitted development for front extensions and alterations, roof changes, porches, gates, fences and walls, and painting. Front hard surfacing is not on the list.

The old core is surrounded on three sides by the Tove flood plain, and the Environment Agency runs a River Tove at Towcester flood warning area. Check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Towcester Article 4 notice (London Gazette, 1998) · Environment Agency: River Tove at Towcester.

West Northamptonshire Council

Council rules and fees for Towcester

  • Dropped kerbs: £330 licence and inspection fee, which you or the contractor can pay; built by only a contractor on the council’s approved list — the licence is issued to the contractor, and the council will not license anyone else. West Northamptonshire Council: apply for a dropped kerb
  • Front driveways: no planning permission if permeable or drained to your own garden; over 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road needs it. Planning Portal guidance
  • Planning: householder applications cost £548 (national fee from April 2026); pre-application advice from West Northamptonshire Council costs £135 for written advice, or £267 with a site visit.
  • Building regulations: West Northamptonshire Council: building control fee schedule. West Northamptonshire’s building control fees include VAT and are from its schedule effective 1 April 2026.
  • Trees: six weeks’ notice for trees in a conservation area, consent for protected trees — both free. West Northamptonshire Council: tree works

How much does a driveway cost in Towcester?

Which council covers Towcester?

Do I need permission for a dropped kerb in Towcester?
Yes. The footway belongs to the highway authority, which in Towcester is West Northamptonshire Council. West Northamptonshire Council only licenses contractors on its approved list to build the crossing (£330 licence and inspection fee). We build the drive to meet it and coordinate with the contractor. Start the application early — it is usually the longest part.
Do I need planning permission for a new driveway in Towcester?
Generally not, if the surface is permeable — resin bound, gravel or permeable block — or if the water drains to a lawn, border or soakaway on your own land. Over 5m² of non-permeable surface draining onto the road does need permission. Parts of Towcester, particularly the older centre, are in a conservation area, where front boundaries and some materials need more care — worth checking before you plan a change.
What is the ground like in Towcester?
Ground in Towcester varies — clay in places and river gravels nearer the River Tove — so we look at drainage on every job rather than assume. Low-lying properties near the river need particular care with falls and soakaways.
