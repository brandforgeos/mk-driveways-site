FAQ

Laser scanning questions, answered

Straight answers about how scanning works, what it costs and what you get. Can't see your question? Ask us.

Frequently asked questions

What is 3D laser scanning?

A way of measuring surfaces in three dimensions by sweeping a laser across them. The scanner records the position of millions of points, building an exact digital copy (a point cloud) of a road, slab, room or garden that can be measured and compared with a design.

How accurate is laser scanning?

Our Trimble X9 is specified at 2 mm range accuracy and 2.3 mm 3D point accuracy at 10 m. Each finished survey also has a registration error, which shows how well the individual scans tie together, and we put that on every report.

How much does laser scanning cost?

Plot checks start from £85 per plot when four or more are scanned together, road scans from £495 a visit, measured building surveys from £325 and garden scans from £395. Every job gets a fixed price first. See the price guide for details.

How long does it take to get results?

Road and plot QC reports are normally with you the next working day. Measured building and garden surveys take three to five working days for drawings.

What areas do you cover?

The whole of the Midlands as standard: everywhere within 50 miles of Corby is covered with travel included, from Nottingham and Coventry to Peterborough, Milton Keynes and Cambridge. We also work anywhere else in the UK, with travel charged separately.

Can laser scanning check a road sub-base against design levels?

Yes. We tie the scan to site control and compare every point with the design surface, then report the deviation and pass/fail against your adoption tolerance.

Can you measure tarmac thickness without cores?

Yes, if we scan the layer underneath before the tarmac is laid. Scanning again afterwards and comparing the two surfaces gives the thickness at every point.

What does a plot or oversite scan check?

Slab flatness, brickwork and blockwork dimensions against the house-type drawing, and drain collar positions against the drainage layout. It doesn't check the plot's position on the site grid or level to site datum.

Is a laser scan survey good enough for a kitchen design?

Yes. It captures walls, openings, out-of-square corners and services accurately enough to design from. Worktop fabricators normally still template after the units are fitted.

Do I get the point cloud?

Yes, with every service, in E57, LAS, RCP or PTX.

Is laser scanning safe?

Yes. The Trimble X9 uses a Class 1 laser, eye-safe under IEC EN 60825-1, so people can keep working around it.

Can you scan in the rain?

Light drizzle is usually fine; heavy rain and standing water are not, because water affects the readings. If the weather is wrong for the check, we rebook.

Who checks the data?

Our team does, on every job. The scanner and software capture and process millions of points, then we go through every report ourselves: we check the scans tie together properly, sense-check the results against the drawings and the site, and flag the problems we find. If something looks wrong we talk it through with you before you rely on it.

Can you check our groundworks drawings before we start?

Yes, on any groundworks or civils project, whether you are the designer, the client or the contractor. The Dimensure Design Audit reviews the drawings for levels, drainage, foundation and cross-drawing problems, and gives you a graded issue log with suggested fixes for your designer and indicative costs. From £495 per site.

Do you set out sites as well?

No. We check and record what's built. Setting out stays with your site engineer, and our checks are designed to sit alongside their work.

Do I need to be there?

On construction sites, someone needs to sign us in and show us the control and the work to scan. For homes, we need access to the rooms or garden; you don't need to stay for the whole visit.
