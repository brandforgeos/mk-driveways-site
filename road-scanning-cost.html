Cost guide

Road and sub-base scanning cost

What a laser scan check of an estate road costs, what's included, and how to get the most out of each visit.

Guide price: a road and sub-base scan costs from £495 for a half-day visit (typically 200–300 m of estate road) and from £850 for a full day. The price includes the comparison with your design levels, a next-day PDF report with heat-maps and cross sections, and the point cloud. Prices are before VAT, with travel included within 30 miles of Corby.

Worked examples

A 180 m cul-de-sac with a turning head

One half-day visit to scan the sub-base, with the design surface supplied as a 3D model. Report the next morning. £495.

A 600 m spine road at sub-base and binder

Two full-day visits a week or two apart. The second scan checks binder levels and, because the sub-base was scanned first, also gives the binder thickness across the whole road. Around £1,700 for both, with the thickness report included.

Three short roads on one phase

One full day covering all three, each with its own report. From £850 rather than three separate half-days.

Compared with doing it by hand

A site engineer with a level or GPS rover can check a sub-base too, and on most sites they will still be there for setting out. The difference is coverage and record. An engineer checks a grid of points; a scan checks the whole surface and leaves a measured, dated record that can be shown to the adoption engineer or used in a dispute. Many contractors use both: the engineer for day-to-day control, the scan at the hold points that matter.

Getting the most from the price

  • Send a 3D design surface or levelled DWG if you have one. It saves the design-surface charge.
  • Have the layer finished and the road clear of stockpiles before we arrive.
  • Book sub-base and binder together and get the thickness report almost free.
  • Combine with plot checks on the same site and share the visit.

Questions we get asked

How much does it cost to laser scan a road sub-base?

From £495 for a half-day visit covering roughly 200 to 300 metres of estate road and footpaths, including the comparison with design and a next-day report. A full day, usually 500 metres or more, is from £850.

Is the design comparison extra?

No. The guide price includes building the design surface from your drawings, comparing the scan with it and the PDF report with heat-maps and cross sections. The registered point cloud is included too.

Is it cheaper to book several layers?

Yes. Booking sub-base and binder scans on the same road together is priced as a package, and the binder scan then gives you layer thickness as well, which is a tarmac depth survey for little extra.

What makes a road scan cost more?

Long runs, lots of bends and junctions, roads busy with plant, 2D-only drawings that need a design surface building, extra deliverables such as volumes, and same-day turnaround.
