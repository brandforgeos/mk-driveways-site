Block paving in Ampthill

What driveways in Ampthill usually involve

Ampthill is a Georgian market town — its market place, pump and clock tower were improved in the 1780s — with the thatched Ossory Cottages, Ampthill Great Park beside it, and newer estates such as Ampthill Heights and Ampthill Gardens on the former brewery land.

Most block paved driveways we design here replace a tired drive or a front lawn on pavement frontages in the centre and estate drives, usually for two cars, with the entrance and the dropped kerb worked out first.

We cover Ampthill (MK45) and the areas around it, including Maulden, Flitwick, Millbrook, Houghton Conquest, Clophill, Steppingley and Flitton. Travel does not change the price.

Ampthill specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Ampthill

The town stands on the sandy Greensand Ridge, yet gives its name to the Ampthill Clay on the lower ground to the north, so ground can change from free-draining sand to heavy clay within a short distance. Because the ground varies, we dig a trial hole and test how it drains before fixing the build-up and the drainage.

Ampthill Conservation Area is large and includes Church Square. Many historic frontages sit right on the pavement, so boundary walls and railings matter to the planners. There is no Article 4 direction.

Sources: Central Bedfordshire conservation areas and appraisals.

Permissions, fees and the dropped kerb in Ampthill

  1. Step 1Check the surface rule: a permeable drive, or one that drains to your own lawn or border, needs no planning permission; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does. Planning Portal guidance
  2. Step 2If you are in the Ampthill conservation area, keep to the surfaces and boundary treatments its appraisal asks for. Central Bedfordshire Council: conservation areas
  3. Step 3For a new or wider entrance you need a dropped kerb from Central Bedfordshire Council: £156 assessment fee, then the council’s quote, built by the council itself. The full Ampthill dropped kerb process
  4. Step 4We design the drive to meet all of this, give you one fixed written price and build it.
