Roads and plots on Northampton's growth sites

Northampton has been growing outwards for years, with large neighbourhoods such as Upton and Buckton Fields and a string of urban extensions around the town. Each brings kilometres of new estate road and long runs of plots, most built to adoption standards that leave little room for error on the bituminous layers.

A sub-base scan before surfacing is the cheapest point in the job to catch a high or low area. Add a second scan after the binder and you have a full tarmac depth survey for the adoption engineer, with no cores needed to find where the thin spots are.

For homeowners, designers and builders

From Victorian terraces in the Boot and Shoe Quarter to 1970s homes in the eastern district, much of Northampton's housing is being extended, opened up or re-kitchened. A 3D measured survey gives your architect or kitchen designer exact existing drawings. For gardens on Northampton's slopes, a garden scan gets the levels right before anyone digs.

Questions we get asked

Do you cover all of Northampton?

Yes, the whole town and its edges, including Upton, Duston, Moulton, Grange Park and the new neighbourhoods around the ring, plus Daventry, Towcester, Brixworth and Earls Barton.

Is there a travel charge for Northampton?

No. Northampton is within our standard 50-mile travel radius from Corby.

Can you scan a road on a Northampton site before adoption?

Yes. We check sub-base and bituminous layers against your design and report against the tolerances in your section 38 agreement with West or North Northamptonshire Council, whichever applies to the site.
