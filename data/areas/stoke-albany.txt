In a rural village, vans do hard work. They carry tools, feed, fencing and materials, and they get used on farm tracks and muddy yards. Eventually one gives up. If you want to scrap a van in Stoke Albany, we buy it as it is and collect it free.

The vans we hear about here come from a few places. Farms with an old pickup sat in the yard. Tradespeople who have upgraded and need the old van gone. Small businesses winding down a vehicle. And households where a camper project or an old work van has stood on the drive for too long.

## Empty it first

This is the one thing we ask every van owner. Please empty the van of tools, materials and stock before we arrive. Check the cab, the load area, any racking and under the seats. Remove any tracker or dashcam you want to keep, and cancel any fuel cards or toll accounts linked to the registration.

If there is a private plate on the van, transfer or retain it with the DVLA before collection.

## How are scrap van prices worked out?

Vans are usually heavier than cars, and because scrap is priced per tonne, weight plays a big part. Other things that matter:

| What counts | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | The heavier the van, the more metal |
| Scrap metal price | Changes with the market |
| Make and model | Some vans carry more value |
| Completeness | Engine, gearbox, catalytic converter, wheels |
| Keys and V5C | Make the sale straightforward |

## Getting a van out of a yard

Vans parked on farm tracks or soft ground for a long time can be awkward to shift. Our collections use the trucks of our sister company, Rapid Recovery Corby, so we can winch a van that will not move under its own power. Tell us about access, gates and ground conditions when we call.

## Paperwork and the legal side

We need to check the seller's name and address, so have photo ID and proof of address ready. If the van belongs to a business, have the V5C and the details of who is authorised to sell it. No V5C? See our guide on how to [scrap without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook).

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. We never pay cash, because the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes it illegal.
