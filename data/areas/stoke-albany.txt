## Why do batteries go flat in a rural village?

A few things make flat batteries more common in places like Stoke Albany:

- **Cars parked outside.** Without a garage, the battery takes the full chill of a frosty night.
- **Cars that sit.** A second car, a farm runabout or a car kept for weekends can drain over a few weeks.
- **Short local trips.** They use more charge starting the engine than the alternator puts back.
- **An ageing battery.** Many fail after a few years, often on the first cold morning.

- Switch off lights, the heater and anything plugged in.
- Don't keep turning the key, as it drains the battery further.

## Making your battery last longer

A battery in a rural car works hard. These habits help:

- Give the car a longer run at least once a week.
- Use a trickle charger if a vehicle will sit unused for weeks.
- Switch off heated screens and the blower before turning the engine off.
- Have the battery checked in autumn if starting has become sluggish.

## How much is my scrap car worth?

The value of a scrap car depends on:

1. **Weight.** Most of the value is in the metal.
2. **Scrap metal prices.** They change with the market.
3. **Make and model.** Some vehicles have more parts worth recovering.
4. **Completeness.** A car missing its engine or other major parts is worth less.
5. **Condition.** Fire damage, heavy rust or serious accident damage lower the price.

Call or WhatsApp us with the registration, make and model for a quote. Collection is free. Our [scrap car cost guide](/costs/scrap-car-collection) explains pricing in more detail.

## Paid by bank transfer, never cash

We always pay by bank transfer. Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, it is illegal for scrap metal dealers and collectors to pay cash for scrap metal, and that includes scrap cars. Anyone offering cash for your scrap car is breaking the law. A bank transfer also gives you a clear record of the sale.

## Licensed and properly recorded

We hold a **waste carrier licence** and a **scrap metal collector's licence**. When your vehicle reaches the ATF:

- The ATF issues a Certificate of Destruction.
- The ATF notifies the DVLA that the vehicle has been scrapped.
- The DVLA refunds any full months of vehicle tax to you automatically.

For a step-by-step explanation, see our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car).
