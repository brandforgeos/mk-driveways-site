## What tends to fail on older cars?

Many cars in villages like Middleton spend their lives on country roads and short trips into Corby. Over the years that shows up at the MOT as:

- Corroded brake pipes and worn brake discs.
- Rust on sills, floor pans and subframe mounts.
- Worn suspension bushes and broken springs.
- Exhaust leaks and emissions failures.

A list of advisories one year often becomes a list of failures the next. When the failures include structural rust, a gearbox, a clutch or a head gasket, scrapping is frequently the sensible way out.

Before we come, take everything out of the back and the cab. That includes tools, offcuts, materials, waste and anything under the seats. We can only collect the vehicle, and items left inside go with it to the Authorised Treatment Facility.

Also remove trackers, fuel cards, dashcams and parking permits, and cancel any toll or parking accounts linked to the registration. If it carries a private plate, transfer it with the DVLA first.

## How much will I get for my scrap van in Middleton?

Weight drives most scrap prices, and vans are heavier than most cars. We also take into account the current scrap metal price, make and model, whether the van is complete, whether you have the keys and V5C, and where it is.

## Can you collect a van that will not start?

## Selling a business van

A business van can be sold by the registered keeper or someone authorised by the business. Bring photo ID, proof of address and the V5C. Payment is by bank transfer, which gives you a clean record for the books. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes cash payments for scrap illegal, so we never offer cash.

## Racking, lining and conversions

Many work vans have shelving, ply lining or a bulkhead fitted. You can leave fixed fittings in place if you do not want them, but mention them when you get your price. If the van has been part converted into a camper, tell us about any gas bottles or leisure batteries, and take loose gas bottles out before collection day. A van with its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels all present is what matters most to the price.

## What happens to the van?

1. We check ID and proof of address, as required by law.
2. We load the van and pay you instantly by bank transfer.
3. The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility.
4. The ATF issues a Certificate of Destruction, normally within 7 days, and notifies the DVLA.
5. Any full months of road tax are refunded automatically.

Cancel the insurance yourself after collection. The steps in our [how to scrap a car guide](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) apply to vans as well.
