If your engine has overheated, don't open the radiator cap while it is hot. The coolant is under pressure and can cause serious burns. Wait for us, or let it cool fully.

## Stopping safely on a narrow, bendy road

The A427 here doesn't offer many places to pull over. If you break down:

1. Try to get the car past the bend and as far left as possible, so traffic can see you in time.
2. Switch on your hazard lights. At night, keep your sidelights on too.
3. Get everyone out on the side away from traffic, and stand well back from the road.
4. Wear a high-vis if you have one.
5. If you can't get out of a live lane safely, call 999.

Then call us and share your location by WhatsApp live location or what3words. Our [what to do if you break down](/guides/what-to-do-if-you-break-down) guide has more detail.
