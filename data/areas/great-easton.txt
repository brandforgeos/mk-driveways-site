## Flat battery in Great Easton?

A flat battery in Great Easton doesn't have to mean a tow. Rapid Recovery Corby offers jump starts, mobile battery fitting and flat tyre wheel changes 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. In most cases we get the car running where it stands and you drive away.

## Why batteries give up in a village like this

Batteries rarely fail for no reason. In and around Great Easton, three patterns come up again and again.

**Cars that hardly move.** A second car, a weekend car or a car belonging to someone who no longer drives much can sit for weeks. The battery slowly drains until one day there is nothing left.

**Short trips.** A few minutes to the shop or the next village never gives the alternator time to recharge the battery fully. Over a winter, the charge drops lower and lower.

**Long stays at the water.** Anglers and visitors at Eyebrook Reservoir can be parked for most of the day. An interior light left on, a phone charging through the car or a cold evening can be enough to stop it starting.

## Jump start near me: what we actually do

### A professional jump start

We connect a professional jump pack and start the engine. Once it is running, we check the charging system so you know whether the battery is likely to recover.

### A new battery fitted where the car is

If the battery is past saving, we **supply and fit a new one on the spot**, on your drive or wherever you are parked. The price depends on the battery your car needs, and we confirm it before fitting anything.

### Wheel changes

A puncture on a narrow lane is awkward to deal with alone. We change the wheel onto your spare or space saver so you can get to a tyre fitter.

## Is it the battery or something else?

Before you call, it can help to notice what the car does when you turn the key or press the start button.

- **Slow, laboured cranking and dim lights:** almost always a weak or flat battery.
- **Rapid clicking but no cranking:** a very flat battery or a poor connection.
- **The engine turns over normally but won't fire:** probably not the battery.
- **Starts after a jump, then fails again a day later:** a worn battery or a charging fault.

Our [car won't start guide](/guides/car-wont-start) explains each of these in more depth.

## When a jump isn't enough

If the alternator isn't charging, the battery will keep going flat however often it is jumped. If the starter motor or fuel system is at fault, a jump won't help at all. We tell you plainly what we find, and if the car needs a garage, we can recover it on the same visit.

## Keeping a little-used car ready to go

If a car in your household only comes out now and then, a few habits save a call-out:

- Take it for a proper run, not just a few minutes, at least once a week.
- Switch off lights, heated screens and the blower before you turn the engine off.
- Don't leave a phone or dash cam charging for long with the engine stopped.
- If it will sit for weeks at a time, a trickle charger keeps the battery healthy.

A car that cranks slowly as the evenings get colder is giving you a warning. Have the battery looked at then, rather than on the morning it finally refuses to start.

Q: Do you collect scrap cars from Great Easton for free?
A: Yes. Collection is free and we pay you by bank transfer.
Q: Will you take a car that has been damaged by flood water?
A: Yes. We collect flood damaged cars from your home, a garage or our secure storage yard and take them to a licensed ATF.
Q: Can I get cash for my scrap car?
A: No. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash payments for scrap, including scrap cars, so we always pay by bank transfer.
Q: Can I scrap a car without the V5C logbook?
A: Yes. We can collect it without a V5C as long as you can show proof of ID.
Q: Will I get my car tax back after scrapping?
A: Yes. Once the ATF notifies the DVLA that the car is scrapped, any full months of tax are refunded automatically.
Q: How much will I get for my scrap car in Great Easton?
A: It depends on weight, scrap metal prices, make, model, completeness and condition. Call with the registration for a figure.
