## Repair or scrap?

A simple rule: if the repairs cost more than the car is worth, scrapping usually makes sense. Some failures make that likely:

- Structural rust, such as on sills, floor pans or subframes.
- Gearbox or clutch failure.
- Head gasket problems.
- A string of smaller items on an older, high-mileage car.

Our guide on [scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair) sets out the decision step by step. Comparing the repair quote with our online price usually makes the answer clear.

## Damp, rust and village cars

Cars in the valley spend a lot of time on wet lanes, and some are parked on drives under trees or in damp outbuildings. Over the years that can mean corrosion underneath, which is a common MOT failure on older cars. Brake pipes, suspension parts and sills are often where it shows. If the tester has marked serious corrosion on structural parts, the cost of welding can quickly outrun the car's value.

## Does the failure reason change the price?

Mostly, no. A scrap price comes from the car's weight, the current scrap metal price, make and model, and whether it is complete. Whether it failed on brakes, emissions or rust rarely changes much.

| No tax or on a SORN | No |
| Catalytic converter missing | Yes |
| Engine or gearbox removed | Yes |

Please empty the van fully before collection. Take out tools, racking contents, stock, materials and rubbish. We take the vehicle only. Remove any tracker, dashcam or fuel card, and cancel any toll or parking accounts linked to the registration.

## Can you collect a van from a farm or yard?

Yes. We collect from driveways, farms, yards and business premises. Every collection uses our own recovery trucks from Rapid Recovery Corby, George's recovery company. If the van will not start, has flat tyres or seized brakes, we can winch it on or use dollies and skates.

The lanes around Great Easton are narrow, and low roads in the valley can flood after heavy rain. Tell us where the van is and what the access looks like when we call, and we will plan the right truck and a sensible day.

## When is a van not worth keeping?

Vans that work on rural lanes and farm tracks take a pounding. Chassis rust, worn suspension, clutch and gearbox wear and tired engines are common reasons a van stops being worth the money. If an MOT failure or a big repair quote has landed, compare it with your online price. For many older vans, scrapping and putting the money towards a replacement is the simpler route.

## Business-owned vans

If the van is registered to a business, the person handing it over still needs photo ID and proof of address, as the law requires. Have the V5C ready. If it is missing, we can still buy the van with proof of ownership.

## Payment

We pay by bank transfer when we collect. Paying cash for scrap vehicles has been illegal since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, and that covers vans as well as cars.

## After collection

The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Keep the certificate for your records. Full months of tax are refunded automatically. Cancel the insurance yourself. If the van has a private plate you want to keep, retain or transfer it with the DVLA before collection, as it is lost once the van is scrapped.
