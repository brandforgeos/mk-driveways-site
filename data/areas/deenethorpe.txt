## Repair it or scrap it?

There is no single right answer, but there is a simple test. If the repairs cost more than the car is worth once fixed, scrapping is usually the sensible choice. Big-ticket fails make that more likely:

- Corrosion on structural parts such as sills and subframes.
- A gearbox or clutch on its way out.
- Head gasket failure.
- Several smaller items that add up to more than the car is worth.

Our guide on [whether to scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair) walks through the decision. If the garage quote has made the choice for you, we are here.

## What does a failed MOT mean for driving the car?

Once a car has failed its MOT and the old certificate has run out, it should not be driven on the road, apart from limited exceptions such as going to a pre-booked test. We don't give legal advice beyond that. The practical point is simple: you do not need to drive it to us. We collect from your drive, the roadside, or a garage forecourt.

If you want to scrap a van in Deenethorpe, the process is the same as for a car. Enter the registration and postcode to [get your instant price](/get-a-price) and the price shows on screen. ## Is my van worth more than a car?

Often a van weighs more than a car, and scrap is priced by the tonne. That said, weight is only one part. We also look at the current scrap metal price, the make and model, whether it is complete with engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels, keys, the V5C and where it is. Racking, ply lining and other fittings do not usually add value. The [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) explains it.

## Empty the van first

This is the most important step with any van. Before collection day, take out:

1. All tools and power tools.
2. Materials, offcuts and stock.
3. Paperwork in the cab and door bins.
4. Any tracker, dashcam or phone holder you want to keep.
5. Fuel cards and parking permits.

Once the van leaves, it goes to a licensed treatment facility. We can't get things back after that. Also cancel any fleet accounts, toll accounts or parking accounts linked to the registration.

## Is it time to scrap the van?

A van that keeps letting you down costs more than the repair bill. Missed jobs and hire vans add up. If it has failed its MOT on rust, the clutch or gearbox is going, or the mileage means every repair leads to another, scrapping it and putting the money towards a replacement is often the better call. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) sets out the questions to ask. If the van has stopped altogether, it can still go; see [non-runner collection in Deenethorpe](/non-runner/deenethorpe).

## Business vans and paperwork

If the van belongs to a business, the person selling it still needs photo ID and proof of address, because the law requires us to check the seller. Have the V5C ready. If the van is registered to a company, call us first and we will explain what helps.

## Paid properly, scrapped properly

We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. We never pay cash. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes paying cash for scrap illegal, and that covers vans as well as cars. A transfer also keeps your business records tidy.

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA. Any full months of tax are then refunded automatically. Our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) covers the same steps for vans.
