A minor failure is usually worth fixing. The ones that tip owners towards scrapping are the expensive, structural or mechanical jobs:

## What happens with an insurance write-off?

If your insurer writes the car off, they will usually give it a category. If you keep the car rather than handing it over, it is known as a buy-back, and you can still scrap it with us.

- **Cat A**: the whole vehicle must be crushed.
- **Cat B**: the body shell must be crushed, though some parts can be salvaged.
- **Cat S**: structural damage, but the car can be repaired and returned to the road.
- **Cat N**: non-structural damage, and the car can be repaired.

Owners often keep a Cat S or Cat N car with plans to repair it, then discover the parts and labour add up to more than the car is worth. That is a good time to sell to us. Our guide on whether to [scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help.

## When a van stops paying its way

Vans work hard. High mileage, heavy loads and steep hills wear out clutches, gearboxes and suspension. Rust takes hold in the chassis and sills. Once the repair list includes big items, it is often cheaper to scrap the van and put the money towards a replacement. Read our guide on whether to [scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair).

## Clear it out first

Before collection, please empty the van completely. Take out tools, materials, racking you want to keep and anything stored behind or under the seats. Check the cab, door pockets and glovebox. Remove trackers, dashcams and sat navs, and cancel any toll or parking accounts linked to the registration. If the van carries a private plate, retain or transfer it with the DVLA before we arrive.

## What is my scrap van worth?

Vans are priced mainly by weight and the current scrap metal price. A bigger van holds more metal, so it can be worth more than a car. We also consider:

| Factor | What we look at |
| --- | --- |
| Make and model | Some vans have value beyond their weight |
| Completeness | Engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels |
| Keys and V5C | Both help |
| Location | Where we collect from |

The instant online price stands as long as the van is complete and as described. If we find a difference, we explain before loading, and you can decide not to go ahead. See the [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) and [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work) for more.

## Collecting a van from a slope or a yard

Larger vans need the right truck. Our collections are carried out with recovery trucks from our sister company, Rapid Recovery Corby, so we can take heavier vehicles, vans that will not start and vans with flat tyres. On Gretton's hillier streets, let us know whether the van is parked on a slope so we can plan how to load it.

## Business vans and paperwork

A company can sell a van to us. The person selling must show photo ID and proof of address, as we are legally required to check them. Have the V5C if it is available. If it is lost, you can still scrap the van with proof of ID and ownership, as our guide to [scrapping without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook) explains.

## Paid by bank transfer, scrapped legally

We pay by bank transfer at collection and never in cash. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes paying cash for scrap metal illegal. Every van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and informs the DVLA. Any full months of tax come back automatically. Cancel the insurance yourself.

## Two vans or a van and a car

If you are clearing more than one vehicle from the same address, say so when you book. Some of our trucks carry one vehicle on the bed and tow a second, so we can often take both on one visit. Each vehicle gets its own price.
