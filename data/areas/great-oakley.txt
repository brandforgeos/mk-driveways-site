## Jump start near me in Great Oakley

A flat battery is one of the most common reasons a car will not start, and it always seems to happen at the worst time. In Great Oakley that is usually first thing in the morning on a driveway in Oakley Vale, or at the end of a long day in a car park at Oakley Hay. Our jump start service comes to you, wherever the car is sitting.

We bring professional jump packs and a stock of replacement batteries. If your battery has simply gone flat, we get the engine running. If it has failed, we can fit a new one on the spot.

## Signs your battery is the problem

- The engine turns over slowly or sounds weak.
- You hear clicking when you turn the key or press start.
- Dashboard lights are dim, flicker or do not come on at all.
- The car has been parked up for a long time without being used.
- Cold mornings make it noticeably harder to start.

If the engine turns over normally but will not fire, the problem may be fuel, ignition or something else. Our [car won't start guide](/guides/car-wont-start) can help you narrow it down before you call.

## Why batteries go flat around Great Oakley

Many of the cars we see here do short trips: the school run, a quick drive to Corby town centre, a few minutes to work at Oakley Hay. Short journeys do not give the alternator long enough to recharge the battery. Over a cold week that can be enough to stop a tired battery starting the car.

Cars left parked for a holiday, or second cars that only come out at weekends, are also common call-outs. Lights or an interior lamp left on overnight will do it too.

## What happens when we arrive

1. We check the battery and connections.
2. We jump start the car with a jump pack, which avoids the risk of connecting to another car.
3. If the battery will not hold charge, we tell you and quote for a replacement.
4. If you agree, we fit the new battery there and then.
5. If the fault is not the battery, such as a starter motor, we can recover the car to a garage instead.

## Flat tyre? We can change the wheel

As well as batteries, we change wheels onto your spare or space saver. If the locking wheel nut key is missing, or there is no usable spare, we can recover the car instead so you are not left stuck. Keep the space saver within its speed limit and get the tyre repaired or replaced soon.

## How much is a jump start in Great Oakley?

Jump starts have a **£40 minimum charge**. New batteries are priced by the battery your car needs, and we confirm the price before we fit anything. You can pay by card, bank transfer or cash. See our [jump start cost guide](/costs/jump-start) for what affects the price.

- The make, model and registration, so we can check which battery fits.
- What happens when you try to start it.
- Whether you have a spare wheel and the locking wheel nut key, if it is a tyre problem.

## After a jump start

Drive the car for a reasonable distance rather than switching off straight away, so the battery gets some charge back. If it goes flat again within a day or two, the battery or charging system needs looking at.

## Keeping your battery healthy

A few simple habits can help a battery last. Take the car for a longer drive now and then, particularly in winter, rather than only short hops around Oakley Vale. Check nothing is left on when you lock up, such as interior lights or a phone charger in a socket that stays live. If the car is going to stand for weeks, ask your garage about a battery maintainer. Most batteries give some warning before they fail, so a slow start on a cold morning is worth acting on.

## Why payment is always by bank transfer

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes it illegal to pay cash for scrap vehicles. That protects you as well as us, because there is a record of who bought the car and when. We pay by bank transfer every time, so please have your account details ready.

## Licensed and above board

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. Every scrap vehicle we collect goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF). The ATF issues the Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA that the car has been scrapped.

Once the DVLA knows, any full months of vehicle tax left are refunded to you automatically. You do not need to apply separately.
