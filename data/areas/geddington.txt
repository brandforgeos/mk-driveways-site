We buy vans and pickups up to 3.5 tonnes, in any condition:

- Small vans for local trades.
- Medium and long-wheelbase panel vans.
- Luton vans within the 3.5 tonne limit.
- Pickups and 4x4 working vehicles.
- Ex-fleet and business vans.

Non-runners, MOT failures, high mileage and damaged vans are all fine. A van that has been laid up in a yard or behind a barn for a long time is a normal job.

Traders often keep an old van going long past its best because it is paid for. When the repair bills start stacking up after each MOT, or a replacement van has already arrived, the old one just takes up space. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help you decide.

## What is a scrap van worth in Geddington?

Vans weigh more than most cars, and weight is the main factor in the price because scrap is valued per tonne. The current scrap metal price, the make and model, and whether the van still has its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels all matter. Keys, the V5C and where the van is parked play a part too.

See our [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) for more detail, or read [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work), which applies to vans in the same way. When you are ready, [get your instant price](/get-a-price).

## Clear the van out first

Please empty the van before collection. We are buying the vehicle only, and anything left inside goes with it.

| Area to check | What to remove |
| --- | --- |
| Load area | Tools, ladders, racking, materials and waste |
| Cab | Personal items, paperwork and chargers |
| Glovebox and door pockets | Fuel cards, receipts and keys for other things |
| Dashboard | Dashcams and sat navs |
| Accounts | Cancel any toll or parking accounts linked to the reg |

## Collecting a van from Geddington

Some streets in Geddington are narrow and parking is tight, which matters with a long van. We work out where to load before we arrive, and we never take a loaded truck through the ford. Our collections are carried out with our own recovery trucks from Rapid Recovery Corby, George's recovery business. Beavertail transporters with winches handle most vans, spec lift tow trucks deal with vans that can be lifted at one end, and dollies help with flat tyres or seized wheels.

Some trucks carry one vehicle and tow another, so if you have two vans, or a van and a car, we can often take both in one visit.

## Business vans and paperwork

You need photo ID and proof of address, which the law requires us to check. Have the V5C ready if you have it. If the van is registered to a company, the person selling should be authorised to sell it. If the V5C has been lost, see our guide to [scrapping without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook).

## Payment and legal disposal

We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. Paying cash for scrap vehicles is illegal under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013. Every van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Any full months of tax are refunded automatically, and you should cancel the van's insurance yourself.
