## The A43 near Geddington: staying safe while you wait

The A43 carries a lot of commuter and freight traffic, and a breakdown on a fast road is the most dangerous situation most drivers face. If your car fails there:

- Pull as far left as you safely can, onto the verge or into a lay-by if one is in reach.
- Put your hazard lights on. At night, keep sidelights on as well.
- Get everyone out on the side away from traffic and stand behind a barrier if there is one.
- Wear a high-vis vest if you carry one.
- If you are stuck in a live lane and can't get out safely, call 999 first.

## Why payment is by bank transfer only

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans paying cash for scrap metal, including scrap cars. That is why we always pay by bank transfer. It protects you too, as there is a clear record of the sale.
