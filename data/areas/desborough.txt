Yes. We collect from homes, roadsides, yards and business premises. Vans do not need to run, because we load them onto our own recovery trucks. Seized brakes, flat tyres and dead batteries are all fine. Let us know about narrow gates or low entrances when you book so we bring the right truck.

## Clear the van completely

Please empty the van before we arrive. That means tools, materials, stock and anything stored in racking. Check the cab, under the seats, the door pockets and the load area. Take out trackers, dashcams and any business paperwork you want to keep.

It is also worth cancelling fuel cards, toll accounts and parking permits linked to the reg. If the van is signwritten, decide whether you want your business details taken off first.

## Pickups and 4x4s too

Not every work vehicle is a panel van. We also buy pickups and 4x4s, including ones used around farms and building sites near Desborough. The same rules apply: up to 3.5 tonnes, empty it first, and have your ID and paperwork ready. If the load bed is full of scrap or rubble, please clear it before we arrive.

## How much is a scrap van worth?

Weight is the main factor, and vans tend to be heavier than cars. The price is based on the current scrap metal market, then adjusted for the make and model, whether the van is complete with engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels, whether there are keys and a V5C, and its location.

| Van detail | How it affects the price |
| --- | --- |
| Size and weight | Heavier vans usually fetch more |
| Engine, gearbox and catalytic converter fitted | Keeps the van complete |
| Keys and V5C | Simpler, quicker sale |
| Location | Factored into the online price |

The online price stands as long as the van matches your description. If anything is different on the day, we explain before loading and you can say no. Our [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) has more.

## Repair or scrap a working van?

If a garage has quoted for a clutch, gearbox, head gasket or a lot of welding on an older van, compare the bill with what the van is worth and how many more years it realistically has. Sometimes the money is better spent towards a replacement. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help you decide.

## Paperwork and legal bits

The person selling needs photo ID and proof of address, as the law requires us to check. Hand over the V5C if you have it and keep the yellow slip. For a company van, bring proof that you can sell it on the business's behalf.

The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA. Any full months of tax are refunded automatically, and you should cancel the insurance yourself. If the van carries a private plate, retain it with the DVLA before collection.

## Bank transfer, not cash

We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, paying cash for scrap metal is illegal, and that includes vans.
