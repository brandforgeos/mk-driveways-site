## Is it the battery?

Not every car that won't start has a flat battery. A quick check helps us bring the right kit.

- Lights dim and the engine turns slowly: almost certainly the battery.
- Fast clicking and nothing else: very flat battery or a loose terminal.
- Engine turns over normally but won't fire: probably something else, such as fuel or ignition.
- Starts after a jump but goes flat again: the battery or the charging system.

Our [car won't start guide](/guides/car-wont-start) walks through these signs.

## Why we pay by bank transfer

Paying cash for scrap metal, including scrap cars, is illegal under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013. We always pay by bank transfer. It also leaves you with a clear record of the sale.

## Our licences and the paperwork

- notifies the DVLA that the vehicle has been scrapped.

After the DVLA is notified, any full months of remaining vehicle tax are refunded to you automatically. You don't have to apply. For a step by step overview, read our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car).
