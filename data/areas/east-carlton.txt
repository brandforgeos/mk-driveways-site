## Why vans get scrapped

Vans cover hard miles and carry heavy loads, so wear builds up. The usual tipping points are:

- Rust on the chassis or structural parts at MOT time.
- A clutch or gearbox that has given up.
- A head gasket failure.
- High mileage, where the next repair costs more than the van is worth.
- A replacement van arriving and the old one having nowhere to go.

Our guide to [scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help if you are undecided.

## Clear the van before collection

Please empty the van of tools, materials and stock before we come. Look through the cab, door pockets, under the seats, the load area and any racking. Take out trackers or dashcams you want to keep, and cancel any fuel cards, toll or parking accounts linked to the registration.

If there is a private plate on the van, transfer or retain it with the DVLA before collection, or it will be lost.

## How much is my scrap van worth?

There is no set figure. Vans are generally heavier than cars, and because scrap is priced per tonne, weight is the biggest factor. The scrap metal price, make and model, completeness (engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels), keys and V5C, and location all play a part.

Enter the registration and postcode on our [get a price page](/get-a-price) and you will see your price on screen. It stands as long as the van is complete and as described. If something is different on the day, we explain before we load and you can say no. See our [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) and our guide on [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work).

## Collecting from yards and business premises

We collect from drives, roadsides, yards and business premises. Our collections run on the recovery trucks of our sister company, Rapid Recovery Corby, so a van that will not start or move can still be loaded. If you have more than one vehicle to go, some trucks carry one and tow another.

We agree a collection time that suits you when we call, so the old van can go when it fits around your work.

## Paperwork for business vans

We must check the seller's name and address, so have photo ID and proof of address ready. If the van belongs to a business, have the V5C and confirm who is authorised to sell it.

Every van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. Payment is by bank transfer, because cash payments for scrap vehicles are illegal under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013.
