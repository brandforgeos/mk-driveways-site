## Battery, starter or something else?

Not every non-start is a flat battery. Here is a quick guide:

- **Slow, laboured turning over:** the battery is weak.
- **Clicking but no turning:** the battery is very flat, or a terminal is loose.
- **Turns over normally but won't fire:** likely a fuel or ignition issue, and a jump won't help.
- **Warning lights all flicker at once:** often low voltage from the battery.
- **Starts with a jump but goes flat again:** the alternator may not be charging.

Our [car won't start guide](/guides/car-wont-start) explains these in more depth. If the problem is more than the battery, we can recover the car to a garage on the same visit. See [breakdown recovery in Lowick](/breakdown-recovery/lowick).

## Why is my scrap car paid by bank transfer?

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash payments for scrap metal, and that includes scrap cars. We always pay by bank transfer. It is the law, and it gives you a clear record of the sale.

## What happens to the car after collection?

We hold a **waste carrier licence** and a **scrap metal collector's licence**. After collection:

1. Your vehicle goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF).
2. The ATF issues a Certificate of Destruction.
3. The ATF notifies the DVLA that the vehicle has been scrapped.
4. The DVLA refunds any full months of vehicle tax to you automatically.

For a fuller walk-through, read our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car).
