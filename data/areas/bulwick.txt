## How do you work out the price?

There are five main things that decide what a scrap car is worth:

1. **Weight.** Most value is in the metal.
2. **Scrap metal prices.** These change with the market.
3. **Make and model.** Some have more reusable parts.
4. **Completeness.** Missing parts lower the value.
5. **Condition.** Heavy damage or rust reduces the price.

Call or WhatsApp the registration, make and model for a quote. Collection is always free. Our [scrap car cost guide](/costs/scrap-car-collection) explains pricing in more detail.

## Why don't you pay cash?

Because the law doesn't allow it. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans scrap metal dealers and collectors from paying cash for scrap, including cars. We pay every customer by bank transfer, which also leaves a clear record of the sale.

## Our licences and the ATF

We hold a **waste carrier licence** and a **scrap metal collector's licence**. Your vehicle goes to a licensed ATF, which issues the Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Once the DVLA knows the car has been scrapped, any full months of vehicle tax are refunded to you automatically. For the full process, read our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car).
