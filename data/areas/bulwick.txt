The quote comes back and it is more than the car is worth. If that is where you are, selling your MOT failure in Bulwick to us is usually the simplest way out.

## Is it worth repairing, or should I scrap it?

A rough rule: if repairs cost more than the car is worth, or the fail is on big items, scrapping is often the sensible option. Smaller fails like a bulb, a wiper or a single tyre are usually worth fixing. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) walks through the decision.

| MOT fail | Usually |
| --- | --- |
| Bulb, wiper, washer | Worth fixing |
| One or two tyres | Often worth fixing |
| Structural rust on sills or subframe | Often scrap |
| Clutch or gearbox | Depends on the car's value |
| Head gasket | Often scrap on an older car |

## No MOT, no tax, on a SORN

If the MOT has expired, the car can't legally be driven on the road except to a pre-booked MOT, and if it is untaxed and kept off the road it needs a SORN. Remember to cancel the insurance yourself.

If you need to scrap a van in Bulwick, we buy small vans, panel vans, long-wheelbase vans, Lutons and pickups up to 3.5 tonnes. Collection is free and we pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. It doesn't matter if the van has no MOT, won't start or has done a huge mileage.

Bulwick is surrounded by farmland, and the vans and pickups around here work hard. Tradespeople along the A43, farm businesses and small firms all end up with a vehicle that has done its last job. We are van scrappers who will come to the yard and take it away.

## Does a heavier van mean a better price?

Weight is the main factor, because scrap is priced per tonne, so a larger van is generally worth more than a small one. After that we look at the current scrap metal price, the make and model, whether it is complete with its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels, whether there are keys and a V5C, and where it is. The [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) and our guide to [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work) explain more.

The price stands as long as the van matches what you told us. If something is different, we explain before we load and you can say no.

## Empty the van first

This is the most important step with a work van. Before collection:

1. Take out all tools, ladders, materials and racking you want to keep.
2. Clear the cab, door pockets and the glovebox.
3. Remove trackers, dashcams and any fuel cards.
4. Take off signage or wraps if you don't want your name on a scrapped van.
5. Retain a private plate with the DVLA first if you want to keep it.

Anything left in the van goes with it.

We collect from farms, yards and business premises as well as driveways. Our trucks come through our sister company, Rapid Recovery Corby, and some can carry one vehicle and tow a second, so if you have two old vans or a van and a car, tell us and we will see whether they can go in one trip. ## How to scrap your van

1. Enter the reg and your Bulwick postcode at [get your instant price](/get-a-price).
2. Accept the price and we call to book a time.
3. We check ID, pay you by bank transfer and load the van.
4. The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility.

## Paperwork for business vans

By law we check the seller's name and address, so whoever sells the van needs photo ID and proof of address. If the van is registered to a business, have the V5C ready and tell us when we call. If the V5C is missing, see our guide on [scrapping without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook).

| Document | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Photo ID | Legal requirement for the seller |
| Proof of address | Legal requirement for the seller |
| V5C | Shows the registered keeper |
| Certificate of Destruction | Issued by the ATF, keep it as proof |

## After collection

The ATF issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Any full months of tax are refunded automatically. Cancel the van's insurance and any toll or parking accounts linked to the reg.

## Is it time to scrap the van or keep patching it?

Work vans tend to get repaired long past the point where it makes sense, because the business needs them on the road tomorrow. If the van keeps failing on rust, the clutch or the gearbox, or the repair bill is heading past what the van is worth, it is usually time to let it go. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help you weigh it up.
