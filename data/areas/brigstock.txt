## Common flat battery call-outs around Brigstock

**Fermyn Woods Country Park.** Visitors leave the car for a long walk or a bike ride, sometimes with a door not quite shut or a light left on. Coming back to a car that will not start as the light fades is not much fun. Tell us where you are parked and we will come to you.

**Cars that sit for long periods.** In rural homes, a second car or a weekend car can stand for weeks. Batteries slowly discharge, and one cold morning is all it takes.

**Short village trips.** A quick run to the shop and back does not give the alternator long enough to put the charge back.

**Winter mornings.** Cold weather makes a tired battery struggle, and frosty starts on exposed driveways are a regular call.

## Battery or something else?

- **Clicking, no crank:** usually a flat battery.
- **Slow, laboured crank:** a weak battery, often worse in the cold.
- **Nothing at all:** a completely flat battery or a loose terminal.
- **Normal crank, no start:** probably not the battery. It may be fuel or ignition, and we may need to recover the car.

## Legal payment by bank transfer

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash for scrap vehicles. We always pay by bank transfer. That protects you, because there is a clear record of who took the car and what was paid.

## Licensed collection and proper disposal

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. Every scrap vehicle we collect goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF). The ATF issues the Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Once the DVLA knows, any full months of vehicle tax left on the car are refunded to you automatically.

## Selling to a legitimate scrap buyer

Before you let anyone take your car, check that they hold a scrap metal collector's licence, that they will pay by bank transfer, and that the vehicle will go to an ATF. If they cannot confirm these things, the car may not be scrapped properly and you could remain linked to it on DVLA records. We are happy to answer any of these questions when you call.
