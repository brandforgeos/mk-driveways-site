## What should you do while you wait?

Stay safe first. On estate roads that usually means pulling over where you won't block others, hazards on, and waiting on the pavement. On a faster road like the A43:

- Wear a high-vis if you have one.

## A jump start near you in Priors Hall Park

If your car won't start in Priors Hall Park, the cause is very often the battery. Rapid Recovery Corby offers jump starts in Priors Hall Park at any hour, along with mobile battery fitting and flat tyre changes. We are close by in Corby, so we can usually reach you quickly.

## Signs it is the battery

You can often tell before you call:

- The engine turns over slowly, or not at all.
- You hear a rapid clicking when you turn the key or press start.
- The dashboard lights are dim or flicker.
- The central locking is slow or doesn't respond.

If the engine turns over strongly but won't fire, the battery may be fine and the fault could be elsewhere. Our guide on [why a car won't start](/guides/car-wont-start) explains the difference.

## Why flat batteries are common on newer estates

In Priors Hall Park, lots of cars do short local trips, to schools, shops and into Corby. Short trips don't fully recharge a battery. Cars also tend to sit for days at a time while one household car does most of the work. After a move, a car can stand for a week or two while you settle in, and that is often enough to flatten a tired battery.

Cold weather makes it worse, because a battery delivers less power when it is cold.

## Our jump start service, step by step

**We check first.** We test the battery and make sure it is really the cause.

**We jump safely.** We use proper equipment and connect it correctly, so there is no risk to your car's electrics.

**We confirm it is charging.** Once the engine is running, we check the battery is taking charge.

**We advise you honestly.** If the battery is weak and likely to fail again, we tell you. You can choose a new battery there and then.

## Battery fitting on your drive

If your battery won't hold a charge, we supply and fit a new one on the spot. Battery prices vary with the make, model and engine of your car, so we price it for your exact vehicle and confirm with you before fitting. No guessing, no surprise bill.

## Flat tyre? We change wheels too

## Can you jump start the car yourself?

If you have leads and a neighbour with a running car, a jump start is possible. The risk is in getting the connections wrong, which can damage the electrics on modern cars, or in jumping a battery that is already failing. On a busy new estate, there is also the problem of getting two cars close enough on shared or narrow parking.

Calling us takes that worry away. We bring a proper booster, we don't need a second car, and we test the battery afterwards so you know whether it will start again tomorrow morning.

## Keeping the battery healthy

A few simple habits help avoid another flat battery:

- Take the car for a longer drive every week or so, not just short local hops.
- Check lights and interior lamps are off when you lock up.
- If the car will stand for a long time, ask about the battery before you park it up.
- Get an older battery tested before the cold weather sets in.

## Why we never pay cash

Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, cash payments for scrap are banned. We pay every scrap car by bank transfer. It is the law, it keeps a proper record and it protects you as the seller.

## What happens to your car after we collect it?

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. Your car goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF). The ATF issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA that the car has been scrapped.

Once the DVLA knows, it automatically refunds any full months of vehicle tax left. You don't need to apply for it separately.
