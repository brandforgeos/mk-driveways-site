## Why newer cars still go flat

Even fairly new cars can end up with flat batteries. Modern cars draw a little power all the time for alarms, keyless entry and connected services, so if the car sits on the drive for a couple of weeks, the battery can drain. Lots of short trips to the shops and schools don't help either.

## What we do

We'll jump start the car and check the battery. If it's healthy, you're on your way. If it's failing, we can fit a new one on your driveway. Some cars with stop-start need a particular type of battery, and a few need the new battery registering. We'll tell you before fitting.

## Flat tyres

We'll swap the wheel onto your spare or space saver, or take the car to a tyre fitter if there isn't one.

A jump start has a £40 minimum charge. See our [jump start cost guide](/costs/jump-start).
