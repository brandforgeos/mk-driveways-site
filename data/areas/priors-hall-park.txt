We buy small car-derived vans, short and long-wheelbase panel vans, Lutons, pickups and 4x4s, as long as they are no more than 3.5 tonnes. Running or not, MOT or no MOT.

## Vans on a new-build estate

Plenty of people living in Priors Hall Park work for themselves or in the trades, and a van often comes home with them at night. New-build streets aren't always designed with vans in mind. Spaces are tight, some developments have rules about commercial vehicles, and a van that has stopped running quickly becomes a problem. Others are left over from a move, bought cheap to shift furniture and never sold on.

Typical vans we buy here:

- A work van replaced by a newer one.
- A pickup with a rotten load bed or chassis.
- A van that failed its MOT on rust or the clutch.
- An ex-fleet van that has done huge mileage.
- A Luton or panel van left over from a house move.

## Clear it out before we come

Please empty the van before collection. That means tools, stock, materials, roof racks or ladders you want to keep, and anything in the cab. We can't be responsible for items left behind. Also:

1. Remove a tracker or dashcam you want to keep.
2. Take off any signage or wrap with your business details if needed.
3. Transfer or retain a private plate with the DVLA first.
4. Cancel fuel cards, toll and parking accounts linked to the reg.

## What is my van worth?

Vans tend to be heavier than cars, and weight plus the scrap metal price makes up most of the value. We also consider the make and model, whether the van is complete with engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels, whether there are keys and a V5C, and where it is parked. The [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) explains how we work it out, and our guide to [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work) covers the basics.

## Loading a van in a tight street

Our collections use the recovery trucks of our sister company, Rapid Recovery Corby. A van that won't start can be winched onto a beavertail transporter, and we can bring dollies and skates if the wheels are locked. Tell us where the van is parked and how much room there is around it.

Some of our trucks carry one vehicle and tow another. If you have an old van and an old car to clear from the same address, or a neighbour has one too, mention it when you get in touch and we will see if both can go in one trip.

## Business vans and payment

If the van is owned by a company, the person selling needs authority to sell it, and we still check photo ID and proof of address as the law requires. We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect, so agree beforehand which account it goes to. We never pay cash for scrap vehicles, which is illegal under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013.

## The paperwork after

Every van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA. Any full months of tax are refunded automatically. Keep the certificate as proof the van is no longer your responsibility. Our [Certificate of Destruction guide](/guides/certificate-of-destruction) explains why it matters, and remember to cancel the van's insurance.
