## Signs the battery is to blame

A flat battery usually gives itself away:

- a clicking noise when you turn the key
- dim or flickering dashboard lights
- an engine that turns over slowly, then stops
- no response at all

## What will we pay for your scrap car?

Every price is worked out for the vehicle in front of us. The main factors are:

- **weight**, because most of the value is in the metal
- **current scrap metal prices**, which move up and down
- **make and model**, which affect demand for parts
- **completeness**, as a car missing its engine, wheels or catalytic converter is worth less
- **condition**, including fire, flood or heavy damage

Collection is always free, so the price we agree is what you receive. Our [scrap car cost guide](/costs/scrap-car-collection) explains pricing in more depth.

## Why we never pay cash

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 made it illegal to pay cash for scrap metal, and that includes scrap cars. We pay every customer by bank transfer. A cash offer is a warning sign that the buyer is not working legally, and there is no guarantee your car will be disposed of properly.

## What happens after we collect it

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. Once collected, your vehicle goes to a licensed ATF. The ATF:

1. issues the Certificate of Destruction
2. notifies the DVLA that the vehicle has been scrapped

When the DVLA knows the car has gone, it automatically refunds any full months of vehicle tax left. You don't have to apply for it separately. Our [guide to scrapping a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) covers the paperwork step by step.
