## What affects the price of a scrap van?

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | Usually heavier than a car, and scrap is priced per tonne |
| Current scrap metal price | Changes with the market |
| Make and model | From the registration |
| Engine, gearbox, catalytic converter, wheels | A complete van keeps its value |
| Keys and V5C | Both help |
| Postcode | Part of the online price |

Your price stays as agreed while the van matches the description. If something differs on the day, we explain before loading and you can say no. See the [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) and [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work).

## Collection from home or the yard

We collect from driveways, roadsides, business premises and yards. If the van is parked on the street outside your house, that is fine. Vans with flat tyres or seized brakes are no problem either. We collect with trucks from our sister company, Rapid Recovery Corby, with winches and dollies for vans that will not roll.

If your business has more than one vehicle to clear, let us know. Some of our trucks carry one and tow another.

## Paperwork for a business van

- Photo ID and proof of address for the person selling, checked by law.
- The V5C, which may be in the business name.
- Without a V5C, proof of ID and ownership.
- Cancel any fuel cards, toll accounts and parking accounts linked to the registration.

Every van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Keep the certificate with your business records. Our [certificate of destruction guide](/guides/certificate-of-destruction) explains it.

## Replace or repair?

A van that keeps going back to the garage costs you work days as well as repair bills. If it has failed its MOT on rust, needs a gearbox or clutch, or will not start, scrapping can free you to move on. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help.

## Paid by bank transfer

Cash payments for scrap vehicles have been illegal since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013. We pay by bank transfer at collection, which gives your business a clear record.

## Vans that have stopped running

A van that has sat outside the house for a few months often will not start, and may have flat tyres. That does not stop us. Tell us about the condition when you book so we bring the right equipment, and the van's weight and completeness still count towards the price.

## Before collection

1. Empty the load area and the cab.
2. Remove trackers and dashcams.
3. Transfer a private plate with the DVLA if you want to keep it.
4. Cancel insurance once the van has gone.
