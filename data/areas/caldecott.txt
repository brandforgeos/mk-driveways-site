### What does it do when you try to start it?

A slow, struggling crank or lights that dim usually point to the battery. Fast clicking can mean it is completely flat or a terminal is loose. If the engine spins happily but never catches, the battery probably isn't the problem. Our [car won't start guide](/guides/car-wont-start) goes into this.

A car left for a few weeks, or used only for short local trips, often has a battery that has slowly run down. It may start after a jump but need a long drive or a new battery afterwards.

Sometimes a jump gets the car going but the battery is flat again the next morning. That usually means the alternator isn't charging or something is draining power while the car is parked. - Interior lights flicker or dim when you start the car.
- A battery warning light comes on while driving.
- The car needs a jump more than once in a short space of time.

We base our offer on five things: the weight of the vehicle, current scrap metal prices, the make and model, how complete it is, and its overall condition. ## Payment by bank transfer only

Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, scrap metal dealers and collectors cannot pay cash for scrap, and that includes cars. We pay by bank transfer every time. You get a clear record of the sale, and we stay on the right side of the law.

When the DVLA has been told, any full months of tax remaining are refunded to you automatically. Our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) covers every step.

Q: Is scrap car collection in Caldecott free?
A: Yes. We collect free of charge and pay you by bank transfer.
Q: How do you pay for scrap cars?
A: Always by bank transfer. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash payments for scrap, including scrap cars.
Q: Can you collect a car written off after an accident?
A: Yes. We collect accident damaged cars from your home, a garage or our secure storage yard.
Q: Do I need the V5C to scrap my car in Caldecott?
A: No. We can collect without a V5C as long as you can show proof of ID.
Q: Will the DVLA refund my car tax?
A: Yes. Once the ATF notifies the DVLA that the car is scrapped, any full months of tax are refunded automatically.
Q: What is my scrap car worth?
A: It depends on weight, scrap metal prices, make, model, completeness and condition. Call with the registration for a figure.
