## Why batteries fail in Rothwell

### Short town journeys

Plenty of Rothwell trips are short: to the shops, to school, to the edge of town and back. The alternator doesn't get long enough to recharge the battery, and over weeks it runs down.

### Cold mornings

On frosty mornings a battery delivers less power. A car that started slowly yesterday may not start at all after a cold night.

### Cars that stand

A second car on the drive, a car left while you work away, or a car parked up for a holiday can all lose charge.

### Old batteries

Batteries wear out. If yours is several years old and starting to struggle, it is probably near the end.

- Dashboard lights dim or flicker when you turn the key.
- The battery warning light comes on while you are driving.

## Why bank transfer only?

Since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, it has been illegal to buy scrap metal, including scrap cars, for cash. We always pay by bank transfer. A cash offer for a scrap car is a warning sign that the buyer may not be operating legally.

## Licensed collection, legal disposal

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. The ATF that takes your vehicle issues the Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. That is what officially ends your responsibility for the car.

Once the DVLA has been told, it automatically refunds any full months of vehicle tax remaining. You don't need to apply.

It is usually time to scrap a car when repair costs are more than the car is worth, when it has failed its MOT on several major items, or when it has been sitting unused for so long that getting it roadworthy isn't realistic.
