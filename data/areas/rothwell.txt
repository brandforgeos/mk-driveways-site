## Failed MOT, what now?

Once a car fails, you cannot drive it on the road unless you are taking it to a pre-booked MOT test or to have the failure items repaired. That leaves a lot of Rothwell owners with a car sitting outside the house and a decision to make.

Ask yourself three questions:

- How much is the repair quote compared to what the car is worth?
- Is this the first big bill, or one of several in the past year?
- Are there advisories that will turn into failures next time?

If the answers point the wrong way, scrapping is often the sensible choice. Our guide on [whether to scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair) walks through the decision.

## Faults that usually tip the balance

Some MOT failures are cheap. A bulb or a wiper blade is not a reason to scrap anything. Others are expensive enough that they change the maths:

- Corrosion on structural parts such as sills, subframes or chassis rails.
- Gearbox or clutch problems.
- Head gasket failure.
- Several suspension and brake items at once on an older car.

Older cars in Rothwell often come to us with rust in the sills, especially ones that have been parked outside for years on the terraced streets near the town centre. Welding can be done, but once a car needs a lot of it, the bill can overtake the value of the car.

Rothwell has a lot of tradespeople, and with the A6 and the A14 close by, vans here cover serious miles. That takes its toll on clutches, gearboxes and bodywork. We often hear from:

- Sole traders whose van failed its MOT on rust or brakes.
- Small businesses retiring an ex-fleet van with high mileage.
- Families clearing a van left behind by a relative who ran a trade.

Whatever the reason, the process is the same as for a car.

## Van scrappers that come to you

We collect vans from driveways, roadsides, yards and business premises. Because we use our own recovery trucks, the van does not need to run. Vans with seized brakes or flat tyres are fine, and we have dollies and skates for awkward spots.

Let us know if the van is in a yard with a narrow gate, or behind a house, so we can bring the right truck.

## Is it time to scrap the van or fix it?

For a working van, every day off the road costs money. If a garage has quoted for a new clutch, gearbox or major welding on an older, high mileage van, compare that bill with what the van is worth and how long it is likely to keep going. When the numbers do not add up, scrapping it and putting the money towards a replacement is often the practical call. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help.

## Empty the van before we arrive

This is the most important step for a van. Take out all tools, materials, racking contents and stock before collection day. Check the cab, the door pockets, under the seats, and the load area. Remove any tracker or dashcam you want to keep, and any business paperwork.

If the van is signwritten with your business details, it is worth thinking about whether you want them removed before it goes. Cancel any fuel cards, toll accounts or parking permits linked to the registration.

## How much is my scrap van worth?

Vans are priced like cars. Weight matters most, and vans are generally heavier than cars, which tends to help. The price is based on the current scrap metal market, then adjusted for the make and model, whether the van is complete with its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels, whether there are keys and a V5C, and the location.

| What affects the price | What it means for your van |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | Larger vans usually weigh more |
| Complete vehicle | Missing parts lower the price |
| Keys and V5C | Makes the sale simpler |
| Location | Part of the online price calculation |

The online price stands as long as the van matches what you told us. Our [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) and our guide on [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work) go into more detail.

## Paperwork for a business van

Whoever sells the van needs photo ID and proof of address, because the law requires us to check. Hand over the V5C if you have it and keep the yellow slip. If the van is registered to a business, have proof that you can sell it on the company's behalf.

The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA. Any full months of tax are refunded automatically, but you need to cancel the insurance yourself.

## Payment

We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. Cash payments for scrap metal are illegal under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, and that includes vans.
