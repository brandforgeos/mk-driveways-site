If you want to scrap a van in Rushton, you can get a price in a minute. Enter the registration and postcode on our [instant price page](/get-a-price), accept the figure, and we will call to book a collection. Collection is free and you are paid by bank transfer.

We buy vans of any age and condition, up to 3.5 tonnes. That covers small car-derived vans, medium and long-wheelbase panel vans, Lutons, and pickups.

## Who scraps vans in a village like Rushton?

Rushton sits between Corby, Desborough and Rothwell, so plenty of people who live here work across the area. Vans tend to be worked hard. When one fails its MOT on rust, needs a new clutch or gearbox, or simply gets replaced, the old one ends up parked outside the house.

We also hear from:

- Sole traders retiring or changing trade.
- Small businesses replacing older vehicles.
- Families clearing out a van after a house move.
- Owners of camper or project vans that never got finished.
- People with a pickup that has been used around a property and is no longer needed.

## Empty the van first

Please clear the van completely before we arrive. Take out tools, racking contents, materials, waste and anything else you want to keep. We collect the vehicle only, so loose items left in the back cannot go with it. Also remove any tracker, dashcam or fuel card, and cancel any toll or parking accounts linked to the reg.

## Getting a van out of a tight spot

Vans are bigger and heavier than cars, which matters on narrow lanes. Our collections are done with our own recovery trucks from Rapid Recovery Corby, George's recovery company. We plan the right truck for the van and the access. If the van will not start, has flat tyres or seized brakes, we can winch it on or use dollies and skates.

The railway line runs near Rushton and some lanes are narrow, so tell us where the van is parked when we call.

## How much is a scrap van worth in Rushton?

A van is priced the same way as a car, but its weight often makes a difference. The main factors:

| Factor | What it means for a van |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | Larger vans contain more metal |
| Scrap metal price | Changes with the market |
| Make and model | Some vans are worth more than weight alone |
| Completeness | Engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels |
| Keys and V5C | Helps confirm ownership |

For more detail, see our [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van). If the van is different on the day, for example the catalytic converter is missing, we explain before loading and you can say no.

## Is it time to replace the van?

Many van owners in and around Rushton reach a point where the old van costs more in repairs and lost working days than it is worth. Rust on the chassis, a failing clutch or a tired engine can all be the moment to stop spending. Scrapping the old van and putting the money towards the next one is often the cleanest option, and our online price gives you a clear figure to plan with.

## Business vans and paperwork

If the van belongs to a business, the person selling it still needs photo ID and proof of address, as required by law. Have the V5C ready. If you cannot find it, our guide to [scrapping without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook) explains what proof of ownership we need.

We pay by bank transfer at collection. Paying cash for scrap vehicles has been illegal since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, so we never do it.

## After the van has gone

The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Keep the certificate for your records. Any full months of tax left are refunded automatically. Cancel the insurance and any breakdown cover yourself. If the van carries a private plate you want to keep, retain or transfer it with the DVLA before collection.
