## Breakdowns on narrow lanes

A car that stops on a narrow lane blocks the road for everyone. Other drivers may come round a bend and find you without warning. Your first job is to make yourself visible and get people out of the car and away from the road.

1. Hazards on. If it is dark or misty, sidelights on as well.
2. Everyone out on the side away from traffic.
3. Wait in a field gateway, on a verge or behind a barrier, well back from the lane.
4. Put on a high-vis if you carry one.
5. Call us with your location. A WhatsApp live location or what3words reference is the quickest way to pinpoint a lane.

If you are on a busier road and stuck in a live lane where it is not safe to get out, call 999 first.

## Battery or something else?

Use this as a rough guide before you call:

- Dashboard lights dim or flicker and the engine barely turns: flat battery.
- Rapid clicking from the engine bay: very flat battery or a poor connection.
- Engine turns over at normal speed but won't start: unlikely to be the battery.
- Starts fine after a jump but goes flat overnight: worn battery or a charging fault.

## Looking after a new battery

A battery we fit will last longest if it is kept charged. A few simple habits help:

- Take a longer drive at least once a week, not just short trips.
- Turn off the heated screen, lights and blower before switching off the engine.
- Unplug phones, dash cams and chargers when you leave the car.
- If the car will stand for weeks, connect a trickle charger.
- If the engine starts slowly as winter arrives, get the battery checked before it fails.

## Payment by bank transfer only

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes it illegal for scrap metal dealers and collectors to pay cash for scrap, including cars. So we always pay by bank transfer. That protects you as well, because you have a record of who bought the vehicle and when.

When the DVLA has been told, any full months of vehicle tax left are refunded to you automatically. Our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) takes you through the process.
