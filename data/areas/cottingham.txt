## Why cars break down on the Cottingham hill

The gradient and bends put extra load on several parts of a car:

- **Clutches** slip or burn out on repeated hill starts in traffic.
- **Cooling systems** struggle on the climb, especially in summer or when towing.
- **Brakes** work hard on the way down and can overheat.
- **Gearboxes and driveshafts** can fail under load on the climb.

If the temperature gauge rises, pull in as soon as it is safe and switch off. Do not open a hot radiator cap. Call us and we will recover the car before more damage is done.

## Staying safe on the A427

The bends through Cottingham limit how far other drivers can see. That makes a stopped car a real hazard.

1. If you can, keep rolling to a straighter, wider section before you stop.
2. Pull as far left as you safely can.
3. Hazards on. At night, sidelights on too.
4. Get everyone out on the side away from traffic and stand well back.
5. Wear a high-vis if you have one.
6. If you are stuck in a live lane and can't get out safely, call 999.

## Flat battery in Cottingham? Here is what happens

A flat battery in Cottingham is one of the simplest problems we deal with, and one of the most common. Rapid Recovery Corby offers jump starts, battery fitting on the spot and wheel changes, 24 hours a day. In most cases we get you going without a tow.

## Where Cottingham batteries let people down

### At home

Many cars in the village are parked on the street or on sloping drives and used mostly for short trips. That never gives the battery a full charge. On a cold morning it gives up.

### At East Carlton Countryside Park

People leave lights on, or a weak battery fails after the car has sat for a few hours. If that happens after a walk, call us with where you are parked.

### On the A427

If the car stalls on the hill and won't restart, don't try a jump start on the carriageway. Get off the road safely first. It may need [breakdown recovery in Cottingham](/breakdown-recovery/cottingham) rather than a jump.

## Signs your battery is the problem

- The engine turns over slowly or not at all.
- You hear rapid clicking when you turn the key.
- Interior lights are dim or don't come on.
- The car has needed a jump more than once recently.

If the starter spins fine but the engine won't fire, the battery may not be the problem. Our [car won't start guide](/guides/car-wont-start) explains other common causes.

3. Check the car is charging properly once it is running.

### Wheel changes

Got a flat tyre? We change the wheel onto your spare or space saver so you can get to a tyre fitter. This is especially useful if the car is somewhere awkward or on a slope.

## Why a quick jump start is not always the end of it

A jump start gets the engine running, but it doesn't fix a battery that is failing. If the battery is old, the car may not restart after your next stop. We'll tell you honestly what we find. If the alternator isn't charging, the battery will go flat again whatever you do, and the car needs a garage. If we suspect that, we can recover it there the same visit.

## Looking after a battery on short village trips

Short runs up and down the A427 rarely put enough charge back into a battery. A longer drive once a week helps, as does switching off lights and heated screens before you turn the engine off. If the car will sit for a long time, a trickle charger is worth considering.
