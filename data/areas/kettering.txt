If the van now costs more to keep than it earns, scrapping it is often the sensible step. Our guide on whether to [scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair) sets out the questions to ask.

## How much is my scrap van worth in Kettering?

Weight is the biggest factor, because scrap is priced per tonne, and vans are usually heavier than cars. The current scrap metal price, the make and model and whether the van is complete with its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels all matter too. Keys and the V5C help, and so does where it is.

## Clearing out the van

Vans tend to hold far more than cars, so take your time before collection day:

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Load area | Remove tools, materials and stock |
| Racking and shelving | Take out anything you want to keep |
| Cab and under seats | Paperwork, chargers and personal items |
| Trackers and dashcams | They belong to you or your business |
| Fuel cards and toll accounts | Cancel anything linked to the reg |

Anything left in the van goes with it. If you want to keep a private plate, transfer it with the DVLA before we collect. Many trades put their business name on a private plate, so it is worth checking this early rather than on collection day.

## Paperwork and payment

The seller needs photo ID and proof of address, which we are required to check by law. Hand over the V5C with the van and keep the yellow slip. If the van is a business vehicle, the person selling it must be able to show it is theirs to sell.

No V5C? Our guide to [scrapping without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook) explains what proof of ownership to bring.

We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. It is illegal to pay cash for scrap vehicles under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, and a transfer also gives a business a clear record.

## After collection

The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction, normally within 7 days, and notifies the DVLA. Any full months of tax are refunded automatically. Remember to cancel the van's insurance.
