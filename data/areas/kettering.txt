## How to keep your battery healthy

- Take a longer drive every week or so, especially if you mostly do short town trips.
- Switch off lights, heaters and chargers before you turn off the engine.
- Use a battery maintainer if the car will stand for weeks.
- Replace an old battery before winter rather than after it fails.

## After we get you started

Repeated jump starts on a dead battery also put extra strain on the charging system.

## Paid by bank transfer, by law

Cash payments for scrap metal, including scrap cars, have been illegal since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013. We pay every scrap car by bank transfer. If someone in Kettering offers you cash for your scrap car, they are breaking the law, and you have no guarantee the car will be disposed of properly.

## Proper disposal and paperwork

- notifies the DVLA that the car has been scrapped
