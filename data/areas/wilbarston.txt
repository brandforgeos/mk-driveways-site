## Flat battery in Wilbarston? Here is what we do

A flat battery in Wilbarston usually shows up at the worst moment: before work, on a dark winter evening, or when the car has been left a week while you were away. Rapid Recovery Corby offers jump starts, mobile battery fitting and wheel changes 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Most of the time you drive off afterwards and no tow is needed.

## Why batteries give up here

Wilbarston sits on a ridge above the Welland valley. Higher ground tends to be colder and more exposed, and a battery loses power in the cold just when the engine needs more of it to turn over.

Other common causes we see in the village:

- **Short trips.** Popping to the shop or into Corby never gives the alternator time to recharge the battery properly.
- **Cars left standing.** A second car or a car left while you are away slowly drains.
- **Age.** Batteries often fail after a few years, and the first hard frost tends to finish them off.
- **Accessories.** Dash cams, chargers and lights left on overnight.

## Jump start, new battery or wheel change

### Jump start

We connect a professional jump pack, start the engine and check the charging system before we leave. If the alternator is not charging, we tell you, because the battery will just go flat again.

### Mobile battery fitting

If the battery won't hold a charge, we can **supply and fit a new one where the car is parked**, on the drive, at the roadside or in a car park. The price depends on the battery your car needs and we confirm it before fitting.

### Wheel change

Flat tyre on a lane outside the village? We change the wheel onto your spare or space saver so you can get to a tyre fitter. We need the locking wheel nut key if your car has one.

## Is it really the battery?

| What you notice | What it probably means |
| --- | --- |
| Dash lights dim, slow turn-over | Battery low on charge |
| Clicking, engine won't turn | Very flat battery or a poor connection |
| Engine turns over but won't catch | Fuel, ignition or sensor fault, not the battery |
| Starts after a jump, dead again next day | Worn-out battery or a charging fault |

There is more on this in our [car won't start guide](/guides/car-wont-start). If it turns out not to be the battery, we can recover the car to a garage on the same call. See [breakdown recovery in Wilbarston](/breakdown-recovery/wilbarston).

## Keeping the battery healthy through winter

A few habits help batteries last on the ridge:

- Take a longer drive at least once a week.
- Turn off heated screens and seat heaters before you switch off.
- Use a trickle charger on a car that sits for weeks.
- Get a slow-starting battery checked in autumn, before the cold arrives.

## Why we only pay by bank transfer

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes it illegal for scrap metal dealers and collectors to pay cash for scrap, and cars count. We pay every scrap car by bank transfer. It is the law, and it gives you a clear record that the car was sold.

## Licensed from collection to destruction

We hold a **waste carrier licence** and a **scrap metal collector's licence**. The ATF we use issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA the vehicle has been scrapped. Once the DVLA has that, any full months of vehicle tax are refunded to you automatically.

For a full walk-through, read our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car).
