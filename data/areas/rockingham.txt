## What fails on Rockingham Hill

### On the way down

Long descents heat the brakes. If they overheat, they fade and the pedal can feel soft or long. If you notice a burning smell or weaker braking, use a lower gear, slow down and stop when you safely can. Don't carry on; call us.

### On the way up

Climbing the hill loads the engine, clutch and cooling system. Clutches slip, temperature gauges rise and older cars can stall. If the engine overheats, switch off once you are safely stopped and do not open a hot radiator cap.

### At the bend at the bottom

The bend near the bottom of the hill is where we attend accidents. Cars arrive too fast, especially in rain or frost, and end up on the verge or in collision. We recover damaged vehicles, clear the road and take the car where it needs to go.

## Accident recovery and insurance work

We do accident recovery for private drivers and for insurance and accident management companies. After a collision we can take your car to our secure storage yard while the claim is sorted, to a repairer, or to your home. If anyone is hurt, call 999 first.

## Staying safe on a steep main road

The A6003 is busy and the hill limits what other drivers can see.

1. Pull as far left as you safely can.
2. Hazards on, sidelights too at night or in poor visibility.
3. Everyone out on the side away from traffic. Stand behind a barrier if there is one, and well up the bank from the road.
4. Wear a high-vis if you have one.
5. If you are stuck in a live lane and can't get out safely, call 999.

Most batteries fail after a few years, often on the first cold morning of winter. A few habits help:

## Paid by bank transfer, as the law requires

We always pay by bank transfer. Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, scrap metal dealers and collectors can't pay cash for scrap, and that includes cars. A transfer also gives you a record of the sale.

2. Notifies the DVLA that the vehicle has been scrapped.

Once the DVLA knows, any full months of road tax are refunded to you automatically. Our guide on [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) explains each step.
