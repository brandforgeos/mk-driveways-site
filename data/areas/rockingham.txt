## Recovering on the hill

A breakdown on a steep hill with a bend at the bottom needs careful handling. We position the truck so following traffic can see it, use our beacons, and load your car so it can't roll. That's what our **PAS 43** accreditation is about: safe recovery in awkward spots.

If you stop on the hill, keep the handbrake on, switch your hazards on, get everyone out and well away from the road, and call us. Tell us whether you're going up or down and how far from the bottom.

## Overheating and clutches

Long climbs can push tired engines and clutches over the edge, especially when towing or with a full car. If you've seen steam, a temperature warning or smelt the clutch, don't keep trying. Pull over safely and call us.

## Accidents

Wet leaves and ice on the bend can catch drivers out. We recover accident-damaged vehicles and can store them securely.
