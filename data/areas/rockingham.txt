- Medium and long-wheelbase panel vans.
- Luton vans within the 3.5 tonne limit.
- Pickups and 4x4 working vehicles.

MOT failures, non-runners, damaged vans and vans with high mileage are all fine. A van that has been parked up behind a barn or in a yard for a few seasons is also a common job for us, as is one with no tax that has been declared SORN.

## How much is a scrap van worth in Rockingham?

Vans are heavier than most cars, and weight is the biggest factor in a scrap price because scrap is valued per tonne. The current scrap metal price, the make and model, and whether the van is complete with its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels also count. Keys, a V5C and where the van is parked play a part too.

Our [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) explains how we work it out, and our guide to [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work) applies to vans in the same way. To see your figure, [get your instant price](/get-a-price).

## Empty the van first

This matters more with vans than with cars. Before we arrive, please take out:

- Tools, ladders and racking you want to keep.
- Materials, offcuts and stock.
- Waste or rubbish in the load area.
- Personal items from the cab, door pockets and glovebox.
- Dashcams, trackers you own, sat navs and fuel cards.

We are buying the van, not what is inside it. If you use a parking or toll account linked to the reg, cancel it too.

## Getting a van off a Rockingham property

Vans are long and heavy, and Rockingham's steep road and stone-walled properties need some thought. Our collections use our own recovery trucks from Rapid Recovery Corby, George's recovery business. Beavertail transporters with winches handle most vans, and a spec lift tow truck can move a van that can be lifted at one end. If the van has a flat tyre or a seized wheel, we bring dollies.

Tell us where the van is parked, whether it runs and anything about access, such as a farm gate or a yard with a soft surface.

## Paperwork for a business van

| You will need | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Photo ID | Driving licence or passport of the person selling |
| Proof of address | Required by law for the seller |
| V5C logbook | Have it ready if you have it |
| Keys | Helps, but not essential |

If the van is registered to a business, the person selling should be able to show they are authorised to sell it. Without a V5C, you can still sell with proof of ID and proof of ownership.

## Paid by bank transfer, scrapped legally

We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. Cash payments for scrap vehicles have been illegal since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013. Every van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA. Read our [certificate of destruction guide](/guides/certificate-of-destruction) to see why you should keep it on file. Any full months of road tax are refunded automatically, and you should cancel the van's insurance.
