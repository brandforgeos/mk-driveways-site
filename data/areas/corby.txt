## Why we pay by bank transfer, not cash

You may still see people offering cash for cars. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 made it illegal to pay cash for scrap metal, and that includes scrap cars. Anyone still paying cash is breaking the law. We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect, so there is a clear record for both sides.

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. ## Should I repair it or scrap it?

It depends on what failed. Bulbs, wipers and a single tyre are usually worth sorting. Bigger items change the sums:

- Rust on structural parts such as sills, subframes or suspension mounts.
- Gearbox or clutch problems.
- A failing head gasket.
- A list of advisories that has been growing year after year.

If the repair costs more than the car is worth, scrapping is often the sensible choice. Our guide on whether to [scrap or repair](/guides/scrap-or-repair) walks through how to weigh it up.

## Can I drive my failed MOT car to you?

We would rather you did not. A car without a valid MOT cannot legally be used on the road except in limited situations, and a car with a dangerous fault must not be driven at all. That is why we collect. Leave the car on your driveway in Beanfield, outside the house in Kingswood or in the test centre car park if they are happy for it to stay, and our truck will come to it.

## How is an MOT failure valued?

A car that has failed its MOT is mostly valued as scrap, so the main factors are its weight and the current scrap metal price. An MOT failure that still drives and is complete is usually worth more than one stripped of parts.

If you need to scrap a van in Corby, we buy small vans, panel vans, long-wheelbase vans, Lutons and pickups up to 3.5 tonnes. Whether it is a tradesperson's van that has finally given up or an ex-fleet vehicle that is no longer worth running, we give you a price and collect it for free.

Corby has a lot of working vans. The industrial estates and business parks at Earlstrees, Oakley Hay and around Phoenix Parkway are full of them, and plenty more are parked outside homes in Kingswood, Danesholme and Lodge Park overnight.

## What vans do you buy?

- Small car-derived vans.
- Medium and long-wheelbase panel vans.
- Luton box vans.
- Pickups.
- Vans that have failed their MOT, will not start or have accident damage.

The limit is 3.5 tonnes. Anything heavier is outside what we buy.

## How much will I get to scrap my van in Corby?

Vans are generally heavier than cars, and weight is the biggest factor in a scrap price because scrap is priced per tonne. The current scrap metal price, the make and model, and whether the van is complete all matter too. A van with its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels in place, plus keys and the V5C, is worth more than one that has been stripped.

Read our [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van) for more on how vans are valued, then [get your instant price](/get-a-price) with the reg and postcode.

Many of the vans we buy in town come from businesses. We collect from yards, units and car parks on the Corby industrial estates. Let us know if access is through a gate or barrier, and who will be on site to meet us.

For businesses retiring more than one vehicle, some of our trucks can carry one and tow a second, so two can go in a single visit. Our trucks are run by our sister company, Rapid Recovery Corby, which also means a van with a dead engine or seized brakes is not a problem.

## Empty the van before we arrive

This matters more with vans than with cars. Before collection:

- Take out all tools, materials and stock.
- Remove racking you want to keep.
- Check the cab, door pockets and under the seats.
- Take out trackers, dashcams and any fuel cards.
- Cancel toll or parking accounts linked to the registration.
- Transfer any private plate with the DVLA first.

Anything left in the van goes with it, so a final look in the load area is worth the time.

## Paperwork for scrapping a van

| You will need | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Photo ID | Driving licence or passport of the person selling |
| Proof of address | Required by law |
| V5C logbook | Hand it over and keep the yellow slip |
| Keys | Including any for the load area |

If the van belongs to a business, the person selling it needs to be able to show it is theirs to sell. No V5C? Our guide to [scrapping without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook) explains what proof of ownership helps.

The van goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA. Any full months of tax left are refunded automatically. Cancel your insurance yourself.

## We pay by bank transfer, not cash

Paying cash for scrap vehicles has been illegal since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013. We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect, which also gives a business a clean record for its books.

## When is it time to scrap a work van?

For most trades, a van earns its keep until the repair bills start eating into the work. A failed MOT on structural rust, a clutch or gearbox on its way out, or a head gasket that keeps giving trouble are the usual tipping points. A van off the road also costs you jobs while it sits at the garage. If the sums no longer add up, scrapping it and putting the money towards the next one is often the practical answer.
