## Why is scrap paid by bank transfer?

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash payments for scrap. Every scrap car we buy is paid for by bank transfer, with no exceptions. It is safer for you, and it means there is a clear record of the sale.

## Licensed and properly processed

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence. Every car we collect for scrap goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF). The ATF issues the Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA the car has been scrapped. Once the DVLA knows, any full months of vehicle tax left are refunded to you automatically.

That means you are no longer responsible for the car once it has gone, and you don't need to chase anyone for the tax.

A: No. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash for scrap, so we always pay by bank transfer.
Q: Will I get my car tax back when it is scrapped?
A: Yes. Once the Authorised Treatment Facility tells the DVLA the car has been scrapped, any full months of tax left are refunded to you automatically.
Q: Do you collect cars that don't run?
A: Yes. We collect non-runners, cars with no MOT and accident-damaged cars, using a winch or dollies if they won't roll.
Q: How much is my scrap car worth?
A: It depends on the car's weight, current scrap metal prices, make and model, completeness and condition. Call 07598 314366 and we will give you a price.
