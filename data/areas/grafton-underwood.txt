## Why batteries fail in a village like this

### Short journeys and long gaps

Cars in small villages often do short trips, or sit for days between uses. A battery that never gets a full charge slowly weakens until one morning it can't turn the engine.

### Parked up for the day

Visitors to the airfield memorial and walkers leave cars for hours. An interior light, a door not quite shut or a charger left plugged in is enough to drain an older battery.

## Is it really the battery?

| What you notice | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard lights dim or flicker when you turn the key | Battery is low |
| A single click and nothing else | Very flat battery, or a starter fault |
| Engine turns over normally but won't catch | Fuel or ignition, not the battery |
| Starts after a jump, then dies again within days | Battery failing or the alternator isn't charging |

## Why we only pay by bank transfer

The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes it illegal for scrap metal dealers and collectors to pay cash for scrap, and that covers scrap cars. We pay by bank transfer every time. It also gives you a clear record that you sold the vehicle and when.

The ATF issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA the vehicle has been scrapped. Once the DVLA knows, it refunds any full months of vehicle tax to you automatically.
