Collection is free and we pay by bank transfer at collection, with no MOT, no tax or high mileage making no difference to whether we will take it.

In a small village, vans usually belong to someone who works from home: a builder, a gardener, a decorator or a small business owner. When the van reaches the end, it tends to sit on the drive while its replacement does the work. We will clear it for you, and free up the space for the new one.

## What decides a scrap van price?

Weight is the big one, because scrap is priced per tonne, which means a larger van is generally worth more. The current scrap metal price, the make and model, whether it is complete with engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels, the keys, the V5C and the location all play a part too. More is in the [scrap van cost guide](/costs/scrap-van).

The online price stands if the van matches what you told us. If not, we explain before we load and you can say no.

## Clear it out first

Work vans collect things. Before we come:

1. Remove tools, ladders, stock, materials and racking you want to keep.
2. Check the cab, door pockets, seat bases and glovebox.
3. Take out trackers, dashcams and fuel cards.
4. Peel or remove signwriting if you don't want your business name on a scrapped van.
5. Retain any private plate with the DVLA before collection.

Anything left in the van goes with it, so please check twice.

## Collection from homes and business premises

## How to scrap your van

1. Go to [get your instant price](/get-a-price) and enter the reg and postcode.
2. Accept the price and we call to book a time.
3. We check ID, pay by bank transfer and load the van.
4. A licensed ATF scraps it and issues a Certificate of Destruction.

## Selling a van registered to a business

By law we check the seller's name and address, so the person selling needs photo ID and proof of address. If the van is in a company name, mention it when we call and have the V5C to hand.

| Have ready | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Photo ID | Required by law |
| Proof of address | Required by law |
| V5C | Shows the registered keeper |
| Keys | All sets, including spares |

## Scrap or keep running it?

If the van keeps failing its MOT on rust, needs a clutch or gearbox, or the next repair costs more than the van is worth, it is usually time to stop. Our [scrap or repair guide](/guides/scrap-or-repair) can help with that call.

## After collection

The ATF notifies the DVLA, and any full months of tax are refunded automatically. Cancel the insurance, plus any toll or parking accounts linked to the reg. Keep the Certificate of Destruction.
