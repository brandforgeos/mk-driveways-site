## How much does car recovery cost?

A straightforward local recovery, such as a car broken down in Corby being taken to a garage in town, has a **£60 minimum charge**. That is the starting point. Jobs that involve more distance, more time or more equipment cost more.

We never leave the price as a surprise. When you call, we ask a few questions and give you the price for the job before a truck sets off. If you are not happy with it, nothing is sent.

## What affects the price of breakdown recovery

Every recovery is different, which is why we price each one individually rather than using a flat rate for everything. These are the main factors:

| Factor | Why it matters | Effect on price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distance | How far the truck travels to you and then on to the drop-off point | The biggest single factor. Local jobs sit near the minimum, long distances cost more |
| Vehicle type and weight | A small hatchback loads quickly, a 3.5 tonne van or campervan needs more care | Larger and heavier vehicles usually cost more |
| Where the vehicle is | A car on a flat street is quick to load. A ditch, field or low car park is not | Difficult locations add time and equipment |
| Condition of the vehicle | Seized wheels, locked steering or accident damage need dollies, skates or a winch | Extra equipment and time can add to the price |
| Destination | A local garage, your home, our secure yard, or a dealer further away | Further drop-offs cost more |
| Storage | If the vehicle stays in our secure yard while a garage or insurer decides | Storage is priced separately when needed |
| Second vehicle | Some trucks can carry one car and tow another on a dolly | Moving two vehicles in one trip can work out better value than two trips |

## Why recovery prices vary between jobs

Two calls from the same street can have very different prices. One car might roll onto a beavertail in five minutes and go half a mile to a garage. Another might have seized brakes, sit in a tight garage and need taking to a dealer in another county.

The work involved is what we charge for. A recovery that needs a winch, dollies and skates, or extra time to protect a low or damaged car, takes longer and ties up a truck for longer. Being upfront about that on the phone is fairer than quoting low and adding to it later.

## How to get an accurate price

The more we know, the more accurate the price will be. When you call or WhatsApp 07598 314366, tell us:

- Exactly where you are. A WhatsApp live location or what3words is ideal
- The make, model and registration
- What's wrong: won't start, lost power, accident damage, stuck
- Whether the wheels turn, the steering works and it will go into neutral
- Whether it is on a road, verge, driveway, car park or off-road
- Where you want it taken
- How many passengers need a lift

If the job turns out different from what was described when we arrive, for example a car that is down a bank rather than on the verge, we explain what has changed and agree it with you before doing any extra work.

## Ways to keep the cost down

- **Choose the nearest suitable drop-off.** A garage close to where you broke down is cheaper than one across the county.
- **Give accurate details.** It lets us send the right truck first time, avoiding delays.
- **Check your cover.** Some insurance policies and accident claims cover recovery. Check before you pay, especially after an accident.
- **Ask about two vehicles in one trip.** If two cars need moving to the same place, one truck may be able to do both.
- **Avoid storage where you can.** If a garage can take the car straight away, there is no storage to pay.
- **Don't wait too long on a risky spot.** A car left on a verge can end up needing a harder recovery later.

## How car transport is priced

We don't use a one-size price list for car transport, because no two moves are the same. A short run from Corby to a garage in Kettering and a collection from the far end of the country need very different amounts of time and fuel.

Instead, every job gets a **fixed price** before it's booked. Once you agree it, that is what you pay for the move as described. No surprises on delivery day.

## What affects the cost of car transport

| Factor | Why it matters | Effect on price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distance | Collection to delivery, plus the truck's journey to the collection point and back | The biggest factor. Longer moves cost more |
| Vehicle size and weight | A city car, a large 4x4 and a 3.5 tonne van load and carry differently | Bigger and heavier vehicles can cost more |
| Runner or non-runner | Non-runners are winched on and off, and may need dollies or skates | Adds loading time, so can add to the price |
| Access at each end | Narrow lanes, low car parks, gated sites or a car buried in a garage | Difficult access adds time |

## Ways to keep the cost down

- **Be flexible on the date.** It gives us the chance to plan the route around other work.
- **Make the car easy to reach.** Move other cars out of the way and clear the garage if you can.

## Is it cheaper to transport a car or drive it?

For short distances with a healthy, road-legal car, driving yourself is usually cheaper. Transport tends to make more sense when:

- The car has no MOT or tax
- It doesn't run or isn't reliable
- It's a classic, prestige or low car
- The distance is long and you would need train fares, fuel and time off work
- You need two cars moved with only one driver

## Common car transport jobs and how they're priced

Each type of move has its own things to think about, which is why we ask questions before giving a price.

- **Auction collections.** The price depends on where the auction site is and where the car is going. Auctions often set a collection deadline, and missing it can lead to storage charges from the site, so book early.
- **Private sale pick-ups.** Distance is the main factor. We also need to know the seller will be there with the keys, and that the car is paid for and released to you.
- **Garage and MOT runs.** Usually short local moves, sometimes there and back. Tell us if the car needs bringing home again so both legs are priced together.
- **Dealer deliveries.** Priced on distance, with business invoicing available for regular work.
- **Classic and prestige cars.** Priced like any other move, with extra time allowed for careful loading of low or valuable cars.
- **House moves.** A car going to a new address, often a long way. Flexibility on the date helps.
- **Non-runners and project cars.** Winching and, if needed, dollies and skates add loading time at each end.

## How new batteries are priced

If your battery can't hold a charge any more, a jump start is a short-term fix. We can supply and fit a new one on the spot, at home, at work or at the roadside.

The price depends on the battery your car needs. Batteries vary in size, power and type, and many newer cars need a particular type to work properly with stop-start and their charging systems. We tell you which battery your car takes and give you the full price **before fitting anything**. If you would rather buy one elsewhere, that's your choice.

## How to get an accurate price

Call or WhatsApp 07598 314366 and tell us:

- Where the car is, ideally a live location or what3words
- Make, model, year and registration
- Whether it has stop-start
- What happens when you try to start it: clicking, slow cranking, or nothing
- Whether the battery warning light was on recently
- How old the battery is, if you know
- Any access problems, such as a garage or a tight space

With the registration and model we can usually tell you which battery your car takes and price a replacement before we arrive.

## Ways to keep the cost down

- **Be honest about the history.** If the car has needed several jumps, it may be cheaper to fit a battery on the first visit than pay for repeat call-outs.
- **Drive after a jump.** A good, steady drive helps the battery recover. Switching off after a few minutes can leave you flat again.
- **Make the car easy to reach.** If it's in a garage, open up before we arrive.
- **Check lights and doors.** Many flat batteries come from something left on overnight.
- **Replace an old battery before winter.** One that struggled last winter is likely to fail when it gets cold again.

## Jump start or replacement: which is better value?

If the battery is simply drained, from lights left on or a car left unused, a jump and a drive is usually enough. If the battery is old, has needed jumping more than once, or the car dies again soon after starting, a replacement is usually the better value. We tell you honestly what we find.

If the battery turns out fine but the car isn't charging it, a new battery won't fix it. In that case we can [recover the car to a garage](/breakdown-recovery), priced and agreed before we move it.

## Signs your battery needs replacing, not just a jump

Paying for a jump start makes sense when the battery is healthy but drained. It is money wasted if the battery is on its way out, because you'll be calling again before long. Watch for:

- The car has needed a jump more than once in recent weeks
- It turns over slowly on cold mornings, even when it does start
- The battery is old, or you don't know when it was last changed
- The car dies again soon after a jump start and a drive
- Electrics such as the stop-start system stop working as they should
- The battery case looks swollen, leaking or badly corroded

If any of these apply, mention them when you call. We can bring the right battery for your car and price it in advance, which saves a second visit.

## What is my scrap car worth?

There is no single price for a scrap car, because the value depends on the vehicle and the scrap market at the time. What we can tell you is exactly how the price is worked out, so you know the offer is fair.

You pay nothing for collection. **Collection is free**, and we pay you for the vehicle by bank transfer.

## What affects scrap car prices

| Factor | Why it matters | Effect on price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weight | Most of a scrap car's value is in its metal | Heavier cars and vans are usually worth more |
| Scrap metal prices | Prices move with the market | Offers can go up or down over time |
| Make and model | Some vehicles have parts that are in demand | Can raise the price above plain metal value |
| Completeness | Engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels all carry value | Missing parts lower the price |
| Condition | Heavy accident damage or stripped parts reduce what can be reused | Lower condition, lower price |
| Runner or non-runner | A car that runs may have more reusable parts | Can help, depending on the model |
| Paperwork | We need the V5C or proof of ID showing you can scrap it | Doesn't set the price, but no proof means no collection |

## Why scrap prices go up and down

Scrap vehicle prices follow the market for metals, which changes regularly. A quote from a few months ago may not be the same today. We give you a current price based on your vehicle when you ask, and we explain what it's based on.

## How to get an accurate price

Send these details by phone, WhatsApp on 07598 314366 or the [contact form](/contact):

- Registration, make, model and mileage
- Whether it runs, rolls and steers
- Any accident damage
- Whether anything is missing, such as wheels, engine parts or the catalytic converter
- Photos of the outside and, if possible, under the bonnet
- Where it is parked and any access issues
- Whether you have the V5C logbook

The more accurate the description, the more reliable the price. If the vehicle turns out to be missing major parts that weren't mentioned, the price may need adjusting on the day, and we explain why before anything is loaded.

## Ways to get the best price

- **Keep the car complete.** Removing parts to sell separately usually lowers the scrap price.
- **Be accurate about condition.** It avoids the price changing on collection day.
- **Have the paperwork ready.** The V5C or proof of ID keeps the collection simple.
- **Send good photos.** They help us price it properly first time.
- **Don't wait too long.** A car left sitting deteriorates, and the market can move either way.

## Scrapping versus selling or repairing

Before you scrap a car, it's worth weighing up the alternatives. A scrap price reflects metal and reusable parts. If the car runs, has a current MOT and only needs minor work, you may get more selling it privately or to a trader, though that takes time and effort.

If the car needs a repair that costs more than it would be worth afterwards, or has failed its MOT on structural rust, a major engine or gearbox fault, or heavy accident damage, scrapping is usually the better financial choice. It also stops the ongoing costs of insurance, tax or a SORN on a car you aren't using.

Tell us what's wrong with the vehicle when you ask for a price. We'll give you a straight figure, and you can compare it with what a garage says the repairs will cost.

## Vans and motorbikes

We buy scrap vans and motorbikes as well as cars, priced on the same factors. Vans are often heavier than cars, so the metal value can be higher. Motorbikes are lighter, so make, model and reusable parts carry more weight in the price. Send the same details and photos for a quote.

## Watch out for these when comparing offers

- **Cash offers.** Paying cash for scrap vehicles is illegal, so a cash offer is a warning sign.
- **Collection fees taken off on the day.** Ask whether collection is free before you agree.
- **Prices that drop on arrival without explanation.** A fair buyer explains any change before loading.
- **No mention of an ATF or Certificate of Destruction.** Without one, you could stay recorded as the keeper.
- **No licences.** A legitimate collector holds a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence.

## What you don't pay for

- **Collection is free**, including non-runners and cars with seized wheels
- The vehicle goes to a licensed **Authorised Treatment Facility**, which issues the **Certificate of Destruction**
- The ATF tells the DVLA, so you stop being the keeper

## How you're paid

Scrap cars are **always paid by bank transfer**. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 made it illegal to pay cash for scrap vehicles. Be wary of anyone offering cash. A transfer also gives you a clear record of the sale.

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence.

## Don't forget your road tax refund

Once the DVLA knows your vehicle has been scrapped, any full months of vehicle tax left are refunded to you automatically. That is on top of what we pay for the car. If you have a private number plate you want to keep, take it off through the DVLA's retention process before the car is scrapped.
