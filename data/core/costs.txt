## The short answer

A straightforward local recovery, such as a car broken down in Corby taken to a garage in town, sits at or close to our **£60 minimum charge**. Longer journeys, heavier vehicles and more involved recoveries cost more. We always give you the full price on the phone before a truck is sent.

## What affects the price

- **Distance.** The biggest factor. A recovery within Corby costs less than one from Kettering to Peterborough, and long-distance moves are priced on mileage.
- **The vehicle.** A small hatchback is quicker to load than a 3.5 tonne van, a campervan or a lowered car that needs extra care.
- **How it's stuck.** A car on a flat street loads in minutes. One in a ditch, a field, a tight underground car park or with seized wheels takes more time and kit, such as a winch, dollies or skates.
- **Where it's going.** A garage in the same town, your home, our secure yard or a dealer across the county.
- **Storage.** If your vehicle needs to stay in our secure yard while a garage or insurer decides what happens next.

## How we keep it fair

You'll get a firm price before we set off, based on what you've told us. If the job turns out to be different when we arrive, for example a car that's actually down a bank rather than on the verge, we explain what's changed and agree it with you before doing the extra work.

## Paying

Pay by card on the day, bank transfer or cash. Business customers and insurers can ask about invoicing.

Need us now? See our [breakdown recovery service](/breakdown-recovery) or call 07598 314366.

## How we price a car move

Every car transport job gets a **fixed price** before it's booked. We work it out from:

- **Distance.** Collection to delivery, plus how far the truck travels to reach the collection point.
- **The vehicle.** Size, weight and value. A small hatchback, a van up to 3.5 tonnes and a classic all load differently.
- **Runner or non-runner.** Non-runners need winching on and off, which takes longer.
- **Access.** Narrow lanes, low clearance or a car buried in a garage add time.
- **Timing.** A flexible collection date lets us fit your move around other jobs, which can help on price. Urgent, same-day moves are possible when a truck is free.

## Examples of what we move

Auction collections, dealer deliveries, private sale pick-ups, garage and MOT runs, house moves, classics and non-runners, anywhere in the UK.

## Getting an accurate price

Send us the collection and delivery postcodes, the make and model, whether it runs, and when it needs to move. That's everything we need for a firm quote. Use our [contact form](/contact), WhatsApp or call 07598 314366.

## What's included

Loading, securing, transport and delivery to the door, with goods in transit insurance while your vehicle is with us. Our transport is open, not enclosed.

Ready to book? See our [car transport service](/car-transport).

## Jump starts

A jump start in and around Corby has a **£40 minimum charge**. That covers us coming out to you and getting the engine started. Locations further out or awkward access, such as a car boxed in a garage, can add to it, and we'll tell you the price when you call.

## New batteries

If your battery won't hold a charge, a jump start is only a short-term fix. We can supply and fit a new battery on the spot. The price depends on:

- **The battery your car needs.** Size and type vary by vehicle. Cars with stop-start often need AGM or EFB batteries, which cost more than a standard one.
- **Fitting.** Most batteries are quick to change. A few cars have batteries under seats or in the boot floor, which takes longer.
- **Setup.** Some modern cars need the new battery registered to the car's system. We'll tell you if yours does.

We confirm the full price before fitting anything.

## Flat tyres

Changing a wheel onto your spare or space saver is priced like a jump start call-out. If you don't have a usable spare, we can recover the car to a tyre fitter instead.

Need a jump now? See our [jump start service](/jump-start) or call 07598 314366.

## What affects the price

- **Weight.** Most scrap value comes from the metal, so heavier cars and vans are usually worth more.
- **Metal prices.** Scrap prices move with the market, so offers can change from month to month.
- **Make, model and age.** Some vehicles are worth more because their parts are in demand.
- **Completeness.** A car with its engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels is worth more than a stripped shell.
- **Condition.** Accident damage or missing parts can lower the price. A non-runner can still be worth plenty.

## What you pay

Nothing. **Collection is free**, and we deal with the paperwork. The vehicle goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues your Certificate of Destruction.

## How you're paid

By bank transfer. Paying cash for scrap vehicles has been illegal since the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, so be wary of anyone offering it.

## Get a price

Send us the registration, mileage and a description or photos of the condition. Use our [contact form](/contact), WhatsApp or call 07598 314366. Or read more about our [scrap car collection service](/scrap-car-collection).

## Don't forget your road tax

Once the DVLA is told the vehicle has been scrapped, you get an automatic refund of any full months of vehicle tax left.
---
Q: Why do scrap car prices vary so much?
A: Prices follow the scrap metal market and the value of parts, so they change over time. We give you a current price based on your vehicle before we collect.
Q: Do you pay more if the car still runs?
A: Sometimes, if it has parts in demand. Tell us whether it runs and its condition when you ask for a price.
