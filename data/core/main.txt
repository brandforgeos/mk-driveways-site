## What affects the price?

The price of a recovery depends on a few clear things:

- **Distance:** how far the vehicle needs to go, and how far we travel to reach it.
- **The vehicle:** a small hatchback is quicker to load than a 3.5 tonne van or a low classic.
- **Access:** a car on a driveway is simpler than one in a low car park, a ditch or a muddy field.
- **Condition:** seized wheels, steering locks or accident damage can mean using dollies, skates or a winch.

## You get the price first

You will always get a firm price on the phone before a truck is sent. If anything changes when we arrive, such as a car that needs winching out of a ditch rather than a simple roadside load, we tell you before doing the extra work. No surprises.

You can pay by card on the day, by bank transfer or in cash. Business invoicing is available by arrangement for garages, dealers, fleets and insurance work. Scrap cars are always paid for by bank transfer, because the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 bans cash payments for scrap.
