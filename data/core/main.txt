## Local recovery, run properly

Rapid Recovery is a Corby vehicle recovery company run by George Mitchell. We've been recovering cars and vans around Corby, Kettering and the villages in between for over five years, and we run a fleet of five-plus recovery vehicles so there's nearly always a truck free.

When you call, you speak to someone who knows the area. Tell us where you are and what's happened, and we'll give you a price and an arrival time before anyone sets off. Most jobs in Corby are reached in 30 to 45 minutes.

## Accredited, insured and careful

We're **PAS 43** accredited, the national safety standard for vehicle recovery and roadside work, and certified to **ISO 9001** for quality management. Every vehicle we move is covered by goods in transit insurance, and we carry public liability and road risks cover too.

That matters when it's your car on the back of the truck. Wheels are strapped, not chained through alloys. Low cars go on a beavertail with a shallow loading angle. Non-runners are winched on, not dragged.

## What we can recover

- Cars, from city cars to executive saloons
- Vans up to 3.5 tonnes
- 4x4s and SUVs
- Motorbikes
- Campervans
- Classic cars

We attend roadside breakdowns, accidents, the A14, A43 and A6003, non-runners from driveways, off-road and ditch recoveries, low car parks and garage-to-garage moves. If you need it taken further, we cover the whole of the UK.

Rapid Recovery is run by George Mitchell from Corby. George has been in vehicle recovery for over five years and built the business around the things people actually want when they've broken down: someone who picks up the phone, a straight price and a truck that turns up when promised.
