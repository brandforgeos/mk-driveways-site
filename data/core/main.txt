## Why do we pay by bank transfer and not cash?

Because the law says so. The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 makes it illegal to pay cash for scrap metal, and that includes scrap cars. Any company still offering cash for cars around Corby is breaking the law, which tells you something about how they handle the rest of the job.

We pay instantly by bank transfer when we collect. You see the money arrive before the truck leaves, and there is a clear record of who bought the car and what they paid.

## The ATF and your Certificate of Destruction

Only an Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF) can legally scrap a car. Every car we buy goes to a licensed ATF, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA. The certificate normally comes within 7 days. Keep it as proof that you are no longer responsible for the vehicle.

Once the DVLA knows the car has been scrapped, any full months of vehicle tax left are refunded automatically. You should cancel the insurance yourself. Our guide to the [Certificate of Destruction](/guides/certificate-of-destruction) covers this step by step, and [how to scrap a car](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) walks through the whole process.

We hold a waste carrier licence and a scrap metal collector's licence, so the car is moved and handed over legally from your drive to the ATF.

## What affects a scrap car price?

| Factor | How it affects the price |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | Scrap is priced per tonne, so heavier vehicles are usually worth more |
| Scrap metal price | It goes up and down, and prices follow it |
| Make and model | Some models are worth more than their weight alone |
| Completeness | Engine, gearbox, catalytic converter and wheels all count |
| Keys and V5C | Having both makes the sale simpler |
| Location | Where the car is collected from |

Condition beyond that matters less than people expect. A car that does not run can still be worth a fair amount, because the metal is still there.
