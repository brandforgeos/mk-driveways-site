## What happens when you call

1. **You call or WhatsApp us** with where you are and what's happened. A shared live location on WhatsApp is ideal.
2. **We give you a price and an arrival time** on the phone. Nothing is sent until you're happy.
3. **A truck is on its way.** Most of Corby is reached in 30 to 45 minutes.
4. **We load your vehicle safely** and take you and it wherever it needs to go: your garage, home, a dealer or our secure yard.

## Vehicles we recover

Our fleet includes spec lift trucks, beavertail tilt-and-slide transporters and a van with dollies and skates. Between them we recover cars, vans up to 3.5 tonnes, 4x4s and SUVs, motorbikes, campervans and classic cars.

## Jobs we're called to

- Roadside breakdowns in town and on country roads

## Accident recovery

After a collision the car often can't be driven and the scene needs clearing quickly. We recover accident-damaged vehicles, store them in our secure yard if needed, and work with insurers and accident management companies, so you're not left arranging it all yourself.

## Safety first

We're **PAS 43** accredited, the national safety standard for recovery work at the roadside. If you've broken down on a fast road, get everyone out on the verge side and behind a barrier if there is one. If you're stuck in a live lane, call 999 before you call us.

## What we take

- Non-runners and cars that have failed their MOT
- Cars with no MOT or no tax
- Accident-damaged vehicles
- Vans and motorbikes
- Vehicles without a V5C logbook, with proof of ID

## How scrapping with us works

1. **Call or message us** with the registration, make, model, mileage and condition.
2. **We give you a price.** We'll explain what it's based on.
3. **We collect it for free** at a time that suits you, from your driveway, the street or a garage.
4. **You're paid by bank transfer** and the vehicle goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF), which issues your **Certificate of Destruction**.

## Done legally

We hold a **waste carrier licence** and a **scrap metal collector's licence**. Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, scrap vehicles can't be paid for in cash, so we always pay by bank transfer.

Once your car is scrapped, the DVLA is told and any vehicle tax you've already paid for full months is refunded to you. We'll explain which bit of the V5C you keep so you're covered.

See what affects the price you're offered in our [scrap car cost guide](/costs/scrap-car-collection).

Q: Do you charge for collection?

Q: Can I scrap a car without the logbook?
