## How the instant price works

The price tool at the top of this page turns a few details into a real figure for your car. Here is what happens at each stage:

1. **Enter your registration and postcode.** We use the registration to look up the vehicle, including its make, model and size. The postcode tells us where it is.
2. **See your price.** The figure appears on screen straight away, worked out from your vehicle's weight and where it is. No contact details and no questions about the condition of the car.
3. **Accept or walk away.** If you are not happy, close the page. There is nothing more to do.
4. **Add your details to accept.** If you accept, give your name, phone number and email. You can also tick a box if you want reminders about your price.
5. **George calls you.** He books your free collection and talks through anything missing from the car, such as the catalytic converter or wheels.

## See your price first, no sign-up

You see your price before we ask for anything else. There is no sign-up and no need to give a phone number or email just to find out what your car is worth.

We only ask for your name, phone number and email if you accept the price. George uses them to call you and book the collection, and we send you a record of the price you accepted. Not ready to decide? You can ask us to email you the price instead, with an optional reminder before it runs out.

We never sell your details. You can read exactly what we collect, why and how long we keep it in our [privacy notice](/privacy).

## What happens after you accept

Once you accept, George calls you to agree a collection time that suits you. If anything is missing from the car, such as the catalytic converter or wheels, he talks it through on that call, before collection. Same-day collection is often possible, though we can't promise it every time. We collect from driveways, roadsides, car parks, garages, garage forecourts, farms and business premises across Corby, Kettering and the surrounding towns and villages, and further afield by arrangement.

Collection is free. On the day, we check your ID, check the car, load it and pay you instantly by bank transfer. The car goes to a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, which issues a Certificate of Destruction and tells the DVLA. Our guide on [how to scrap a car legally](/guides/how-to-scrap-a-car) covers each step.

## Will the final price change on the day?

The price stands as long as the car is complete and as described. It only changes if something is different when we arrive. Common examples:

- The catalytic converter has been removed.
- The engine, gearbox or wheels are missing.
- The car is a different vehicle or has been altered since you got the price.

If anything is different, we explain why before we load and you can say no. Telling George about anything missing when he calls is the simplest way to make sure the figure holds. Our guide on [how scrap car prices work](/guides/how-scrap-car-prices-work) explains what each factor does.

## What to have ready

To make collection day quick and straightforward:

- Photo ID, such as a driving licence or passport.
- Proof of address.
- The V5C logbook, if you have it. If not, proof of ownership. See [scrapping without a logbook](/guides/scrap-car-without-logbook).
- The keys, including any spare.
- An empty car, with the glovebox, door pockets, boot and spare wheel well checked.

If you want to keep a personalised number plate, transfer or retain it with the DVLA before the car is scrapped.

## Who we are

This website and its instant price tool are run by George Mitchell trading as We Buy Scrap Cars Corby. George is the data controller for the personal information described here.

If you have any questions about this notice or how your information is used, contact George by email at info@webuyscrapcars-corby.co.uk or by phone on 07598 314366.

## What information we collect

When you use our [instant price tool](/get-a-price), we use the vehicle's registration number and the postcode where it is to work out your price. We also look up vehicle details from the registration, such as make, model and size. You don't need to give any contact details to see a price.

We only collect your name, phone number and email address if you accept a price or contact us through the contact form. If you ask us to email you your price, we only collect your email address. In that case we may also hold:

- Your name, phone number and email address.
- The vehicle's registration number and postcode.
- Anything you tell us about the vehicle when we speak, such as whether it is complete, has keys or has a V5C.
- The price we offered you, and whether you accepted it.

When we collect a vehicle, we also record the seller's ID details, as the law requires.

## Why we use it

We use your information to:

- **Give you a price and honour it.** We use the registration and postcode to work out a price. If you accept, we keep a record so the price stands when we collect.
- **Contact you about your collection.** If you accept, we use your phone number and email to arrange a time, send you a record of the price and keep you updated.
- **Reply to your message.** If you use the contact form, we use your details to get back to you.
- **Meet our legal duty.** The Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 requires us to record sellers' ID details at collection.
- **Send you a reminder about your price** shortly before it runs out, but only if you tick the box to say you want one. Every reminder has a link to stop them.

## Our lawful bases

Data protection law requires us to have a lawful basis for each use:

| Use | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Working out and giving you a price, and arranging collection | Taking steps at your request before entering a contract |
| Recording seller ID details at collection | Legal obligation |
| Following up on your own enquiry | Legitimate interests |
| Sending reminders about your price | Consent |

## Who we share it with

We only share your information where it is needed to provide the service:

- **The vehicle data provider** we use to look up the registration.
- **Our email provider**, which handles messages we send and receive.
- **Our web host**, which runs the website and price tool.
- **The Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF)** that scraps the vehicle, so it can complete the scrapping and notify the DVLA.

We never sell your information, and we don't pass it to anyone for marketing.

## How long we keep it

- **Enquiries that don't go ahead:** 12 months, so we can help if you come back to us about the same vehicle.
- **Completed collections:** as long as the law requires for scrap metal records.

After that, we delete or securely dispose of the information.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** information that is wrong or incomplete.
- **Ask us to delete** your information, unless we must keep it by law.
- **Object** to us using your information based on legitimate interests.
- **Withdraw consent** for reminders at any time. This doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, contact George at info@webuyscrapcars-corby.co.uk or 07598 314366. We will respond as quickly as we can.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

## Cookies

This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies. We don't follow you around the internet or build a profile of you for advertising.

## Keeping your information safe

We only collect what we need to price and collect your vehicle, and we only share it with the providers listed above. Access to your information is limited to what is needed to run the service.
