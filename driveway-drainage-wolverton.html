Driveway drainage in Wolverton

Driveway drainage in Wolverton

Wolverton was laid out as a Victorian railway town, and parking still follows that plan. Many terraced houses sit right on the pavement with nothing in front, later streets have a low brick wall and a small yard, and the back ways serve the rear. So drainage problems here tend to be in small, hard spaces: a front yard that holds water against the wall, or a rear hardstanding that drains back towards the house.

Small spaces need precise levels. On a short yard there is little room to build in fall, so we check the existing gradients carefully and set any channel where it catches water before it reaches the door. The water then has to go somewhere legal, which rules out the foul sewer and the road. Wolverton specifically

What makes Wolverton different

Ground in Wolverton changes from street to street. The town sits on Great Oolite limestone, the local Blisworth stone, but patches of boulder clay lie over it, so one garden may drain well while the next holds water. We test before relying on a soakaway. On the road-facing side of houses in the conservation area, the Article 4 direction removes permitted development for any new hard surface at the front, so we check that before designing anything there.

A typical Wolverton job is a terrace or villa with a small front yard behind a low brick wall, or a parking space off the back way. Work usually means lifting a strip of paving, fitting a short heavy-duty channel at the low point and running it to a tested soakaway, adjusting the falls so water stops pooling at the threshold.

Questions

Driveway drainage in Wolverton: questions

What does drainage cost for a small Wolverton yard?
Short runs are priced by the metre, at £120–£200 for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel. Running one right across a typical driveway or garage entrance comes to about £1,000–£1,800 installed. If a soakaway is needed, we price it after a percolation test, and your fixed price comes in writing after the visit.
Does Wolverton's Article 4 direction affect drive drainage?
The drainage itself needs no planning permission when it is on your own driveway. But in the conservation area the Article 4 direction covers the road-facing side of houses, including front walls and any new hard surface at the front. If a fix involves either, that part needs planning permission. Rear gardens off the back ways are not affected.
Can a soakaway work in a Wolverton back garden?
Sometimes. Where the Blisworth limestone is close to the surface, ground can drain reasonably well; where boulder clay sits on top, it may not. Because that changes from street to street, we dig a test hole and run a percolation test before we design or price any soakaway.
My parking space is off the back way. Where can the water go?
It has to stay out of the foul sewer and off the road. Depending on the plot, that means a soakaway in the garden, a permeable area, or a surface water sewer if the water company approves the connection. We look at levels and space at the visit and tell you which is realistic.
