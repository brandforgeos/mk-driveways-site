Newport Pagnell specifically

Homes, ground and conservation in Newport Pagnell

Newport Pagnell has a Georgian High Street on old burgage plots, Victorian villas and terraces on streets such as Mill Street, Silver Street and Tickford Street, and estates built from the 1980s onwards; the neighbourhood plan allocates around 1,400 more homes to 2031.

The centre stands on a knoll of Oxford Clay that grades down to Kellaways sands, river gravels and alluvium beside the Ouse and Ouzel. How well the ground drains changes sharply over a short distance, and groundwater is higher near the rivers.

Newport Pagnell Conservation Area covers the old town. Its review asks owners not to lose front gardens to hard standing and to keep brick boundary walls with their triangular or half-round copings. At Castle Meadow a separate Article 4 direction covers walls, fences and gates.

The town sits above the confluence of the Great Ouse and Ouzel. Land either side of both rivers is liable to flood, and the Environment Agency issues flood warnings for the low-lying areas. Check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Newport Pagnell Conservation Area review.

  • Planning: householder applications cost £548 (national fee from April 2026); pre-application advice from Milton Keynes City Council costs £360 for a householder project (£180 for outbuildings), with a written reply within six weeks.
  • Building regulations: Milton Keynes City Council: building regulation charges. Building control figures are from the council’s 2026 scheme of charges; confirm the current figure with Building Control before you apply.

Do I need permission for a dropped kerb in Newport Pagnell?
Yes. The footway belongs to the highway authority, which in Newport Pagnell is Milton Keynes City Council. You apply for the licence as the homeowner; we tell you what they need, provide the drawings and carry out the crossing once it is approved. Start the application early — it is usually the longest part.
Do I need planning permission for a new driveway in Newport Pagnell?
Generally not, if the surface is permeable — resin bound, gravel or permeable block — or if the water drains to a lawn, border or soakaway on your own land. Over 5m² of non-permeable surface draining onto the road does need permission. Parts of Newport Pagnell, particularly the older centre, are in a conservation area, where front boundaries and some materials need more care — worth checking before you plan a change.
What is the ground like in Newport Pagnell?
Ground in Newport Pagnell varies — clay in places and river gravels nearer the Ouse and Ouzel — so we look at drainage on every job rather than assume. Low-lying properties near the river need particular care with falls and soakaways.
