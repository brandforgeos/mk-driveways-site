## Safety advice you can give (accurate UK guidance)
- On a fast road: pull as far left/onto the verge or into a lay-by as you safely can, hazards on, everyone out on the side away from traffic, stand behind a barrier if there is one, wear a high-vis if you have one. If stuck in a live lane and unable to get out safely, call 999.
- Don't restart an engine that has stalled in floodwater.
- Don't open a hot radiator cap.
- At night: sidelights on, hazards on.
- Share location: WhatsApp live location, or what3words, or nearest junction/marker post/landmark and direction of travel.
